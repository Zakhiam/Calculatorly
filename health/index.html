Questions about Health and Fitness calculators

Are the health calculators medical advice?

No. They are general guides only, worked out from the numbers you type. They never replace advice from a doctor or another health professional.

Can I use metric or imperial units?

Yes. Health tools accept both systems where it matters — kilograms and pounds, centimetres and inches — so you can work in the units you know.

Is my health information stored?

No. The calculation runs in your browser on your own device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere or stored.

Are the health tools free?

Yes. Every calculator on this site is free, with no sign-up.
