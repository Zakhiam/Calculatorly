2Quick answer

The due date is about 280 days, or 40 weeks, after the first day of the last period. If that day was 4 February 2026, the due date is 11 November 2026. Type your date above and this calculator shows the due date, the trimesters and the milestone dates.

3The formula

The trimesters are counted from the same date: the first trimester runs to 13 weeks and 6 days, the second to 27 weeks and 6 days, and the third runs to the due date. Scans and checks are dated from it too.

Due date — in words: add 280 days to the first day of the last period. due date = first day of last period + 280 days

Formula source: Naegele's rule, 280 days from the first day of the last period, on the date you type. This is an estimate only, not medical advice — only about 1 in 20 babies arrives on the due date.

6Questions about pregnancy planner

Is the due date exact?

No. It is an estimate. Only about 1 in 20 babies arrives on the due date, and most arrive within two weeks either side of it. A scan in early pregnancy can move the date by a few days.

What if I do not know the first day of my last period?

Then this page cannot give a date. An early scan is the usual way to date the pregnancy when the last period is not known — ask your doctor or midwife.

What are the trimesters?

The first trimester is weeks 1 to 13, the second is weeks 14 to 27, and the third is week 28 to the birth. They are counted from the first day of the last period, which is about two weeks before conception.

When is the 12-week scan?

About 12 weeks after the first day of the last period, which is 84 days on. For a last period of 4 February 2026 that is 29 April 2026. The table under your result shows it.

Is this medical advice?

No. This is an estimate only, not medical advice. It never replaces a doctor or midwife, and it cannot tell you anything about the health of the pregnancy.

Is this calculator free, and do I need to sign up?

Yes, it is free, and no sign-up is needed. Nothing is uploaded: the calculation runs in your browser on your own device, and the date you type is not sent anywhere.
