2Quick answer

Body Mass Index is your weight divided by your height squared. For example, 70 kilograms at 175 centimetres gives a BMI of 22.9, which is in the healthy weight range. Type your weight and height above, in metric or imperial units, and this calculator gives your BMI, its category and the healthy weight range for your height.

3The formula

BMI compares weight with height, so taller people are not judged by the same weight as shorter people. The formula is:

BMI — in words: divide your weight in kilograms by your height in metres, squared. In imperial units, multiply your weight in pounds by 703 and divide by your height in inches, squared. BMI = weight in kg ÷ (height in m)², or BMI = 703 × weight in lb ÷ (height in in)²

Formula source: the standard Body Mass Index definition (weight divided by height squared), with the World Health Organization category cut-offs: under 18.5 underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 healthy weight, 25 to 29.9 overweight, 30 and over obesity. This is a guide only, not medical advice.

4Worked example: 70 kg at 175 cm

  1. Start with the figures: weight 70 kg, height 175 cm.
  2. Turn the height into metres: 175 cm = 1.75 m, and square it: 1.75 × 1.75 = 3.0625.
  3. Divide the weight by that number: 70 ÷ 3.0625 = 22.857.
  4. Answer: the BMI is 22.9, which is in the healthy weight range (18.5 to 24.9).

Try it in the tool above: type 70 and 175 with Metric picked, and the result line shows 22.9.

6Questions about bmi

What is a healthy BMI?

A BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is classed as a healthy weight. Under 18.5 is underweight, 25 to 29.9 is overweight, and 30 and over is obesity. These are the World Health Organization cut-offs for adults.

Is BMI the same for everyone?

No. BMI does not measure body fat, muscle or bone, so it can mislead for athletes with a lot of muscle, older people, children and pregnant women. For children, BMI is judged against growth charts, not the adult cut-offs.

How do I work out BMI in pounds and inches?

Multiply your weight in pounds by 703, then divide by your height in inches squared. For example, 154 lb at 69 in: 703 × 154 ÷ (69 × 69) = 22.7. Pick Imperial above and the calculator does it for you.

What weight is healthy for my height?

Multiply 18.5 and 24.9 by your height in metres squared. At 175 cm that is 56.7 to 76.3 kg. The table under your result shows the range for the height you typed.

Can BMI tell me if I am healthy?

Not on its own. BMI is one rough guide to weight for height. Blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol, fitness and how you feel all matter too. If your BMI is outside the healthy range, talk to a doctor rather than acting on this number alone.

How is the BMI rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 1 decimal place. For example, 70 kg at 175 cm gives 22.857, which rounds to 22.9. The same rule is used in the answer, the bar and the table, so the figures always agree.
