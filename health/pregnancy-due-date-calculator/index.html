2Quick answer

If the first day of your last period was 3 August 2026, the estimated due date is 10 May 2027. The date is the first day of the last period plus 280 days, which is 40 weeks. This is an estimate only, not medical advice, and only a scan can date a pregnancy exactly.

3The formula

Pregnancy is counted as 40 weeks from the first day of the last period, even though conception happens about two weeks later. The rule is called Naegele's rule:

Due date — in words: add 280 days, which is 40 weeks, to the first day of the last period. due date = first day of last period + 280 days

Formula source: Naegele's rule, the standard 280-day count used in pregnancy care. This is an estimate only, not medical advice — only a scan and a doctor can date a pregnancy exactly, and few babies arrive on the exact due date.

6Questions about pregnancy due date

Why 280 days?

Pregnancy is counted as 40 weeks from the first day of the last period. Conception usually happens about two weeks after that date, so the baby is really about 38 weeks old at the due date — but the 40-week count is the standard doctors use.

How exact is the due date?

It is an estimate. Only about 1 in 20 babies arrive on the exact due date, and most arrive within two weeks either side. A scan in early pregnancy dates the pregnancy more exactly than this count.

What if I do not know the first day of my last period?

Then this calculator cannot give a date — the whole sum starts from that day. Ask your doctor, who can date the pregnancy from a scan instead.

What does the week of pregnancy mean?

It is the number of full weeks from the first day of the last period to today. The table under your result shows it, with the trimester: weeks 1–13 are the first, 14–27 the second, and 28–40 the third.

What if the date I pick is in the future?

A last period cannot start in the future, so the calculator shows a clear message instead of a due date. Pick a date in the past.

Is this medical advice?

No. This is an estimate only, not medical advice. It never replaces care from a doctor or a midwife — talk to them about your pregnancy, your due date and any worries.
