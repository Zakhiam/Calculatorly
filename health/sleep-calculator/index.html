2Quick answer

Sleep runs in cycles of about 90 minutes, and waking at the end of a cycle feels easier than waking in the middle of one. To wake at 7:00 after 5 cycles and 15 minutes to fall asleep, go to sleep at 23:15. Type your wake time above and this calculator shows the best bedtimes.

3The formula

Five cycles give 7.5 hours of sleep, six give 9 hours, and four give 6 hours. The result line shows the bedtime for 5 cycles, which suits most adults.

Best bedtime — in words: take the wake time, take off the minutes to fall asleep, then take off 90 minutes for each sleep cycle. bedtime = wake time − minutes to fall asleep − (sleep cycles × 90 minutes)

Formula source: 90-minute sleep cycles, on the two numbers you type. This is a general guide only, not medical advice.

4Worked example: wake at 7:00, 15 minutes to fall asleep, 5 cycles

  1. Sleep needed: 5 cycles × 90 minutes = 450 minutes, or 7.5 hours.
  2. Count back from 7:00: take off 15 minutes to fall asleep, which gives 6:45.
  3. Take off 450 minutes of sleep: 6:45 minus 7.5 hours = 23:15.
  4. Answer: 23:15. For 6 cycles the bedtime is 21:45, and for 4 cycles it is 00:45.

Try it in the tool above: type 07:00 and 15, and the result line shows 23:15.

6Questions about sleep

Why 90-minute cycles?

Sleep moves through light sleep, deep sleep and dreaming in cycles of about 90 minutes. Waking at the end of a cycle usually feels easier than waking in the middle of deep sleep.

How many cycles do I need?

Most adults need 5 or 6 cycles, which is 7.5 to 9 hours of sleep. Four cycles, or 6 hours, is the least shown here, and most adults feel tired on that for long.

What if it takes me longer to fall asleep?

Put your usual time in the minutes box. If it takes 30 minutes, the bedtime moves 15 minutes earlier than the example. The calculator counts it back from the wake time.

Does this work for naps?

Not really. A nap is usually one cycle or less. This page is made for a full night of sleep, counted back from a wake time.

Is this medical advice?

No. This is a general guide only, worked out from the numbers you type. If you often cannot sleep, or you feel tired every day, talk to a doctor.

Is this calculator free, and do I need to sign up?

Yes, it is free, and no sign-up is needed. Nothing is uploaded: the calculation runs in your browser on your own device, and the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
