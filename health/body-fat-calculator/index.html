2Quick answer

A man of 180 cm with a 90 cm waist and a 40 cm neck has about 18.4% body fat by the US Navy method. It uses height, waist and neck for men, and adds the hip for women. It is a guide only, from the numbers you type — not medical advice, and never a replacement for a doctor.

3The formula

The US Navy method estimates body fat from tape measurements instead of a scan. All measurements are in centimetres:

Body fat (US Navy method) — in words: the result comes from the logs of the waist, neck and height measurements for men, with the hip added for women. men: 495 ÷ (1.0324 − 0.19077 × log10(waist − neck) + 0.15456 × log10(height)) − 450; women: 495 ÷ (1.29579 − 0.35004 × log10(waist + hip − neck) + 0.22100 × log10(height)) − 450

Formula source: the US Navy body fat formula, the version used by the US Department of Defense. It is an estimate, not a scan, and it can be off by a few percent for very muscular or very lean people.

4Worked example: man, 180 cm, waist 90 cm, neck 40 cm

  1. Start with the numbers: male, height 180 cm, waist 90 cm, neck 40 cm.
  2. Waist minus neck is 90 − 40 = 50 cm. The log of 50 is 1.6990 and the log of 180 is 2.2553.
  3. Put the numbers in the formula: 495 ÷ (1.0324 − 0.19077 × 1.6990 + 0.15456 × 2.2553) − 450 = 495 ÷ 1.0569 − 450 = 18.35.
  4. Answer: the body fat is 18.4% (rounded half-up to 1 decimal place).

Try it in the tool above: choose male and type 30, 180, 90, 40 and 0, and the result line shows 18.4%.

6Questions about body fat

How exact is the US Navy method?

It is an estimate, usually within a few percent of a scan. It works best for average builds. Very muscular people can read higher than they are, because muscle makes the neck and waist thicker, and very lean people can read lower.

Why do women need a hip measurement?

Because women carry more fat around the hips, so the hip size carries real information about body fat. For men the waist and neck are enough, so the hip box is left at 0.

How should I measure my waist?

Stand relaxed and measure around the waist at the navel, with the tape level and snug but not tight. Do not breathe in. Measure twice and use the average if the two readings differ.

What is a healthy body fat percent?

For men, roughly 10–20% is the fitness range and 21–24% is average; for women, roughly 16–24% is the fitness range and 25–31% is average. These are guides, not medical limits, and age and sport change them.

Why does the calculator stop when the waist is smaller than the neck?

Because the formula takes the log of waist minus neck, and the log of zero or a negative number has no answer. A waist smaller than the neck is a measuring mistake — check the tape and try again.

Is this medical advice?

No. This is a general guide only, worked out from the numbers you type. It is not a diagnosis and never replaces advice from a doctor or another health professional.
