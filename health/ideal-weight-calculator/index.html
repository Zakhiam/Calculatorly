2Quick answer

For a height of 175 cm, the healthy weight range is 56.7–76.3 kg, which is 124.9–168.1 lb. The range comes from the healthy body mass index range of 18.5 to 24.9. The Devine ideal weight for a man of this height is 70.5 kg. This is a guide only, not medical advice.

3The formula

Body mass index is weight divided by height squared. Turning the healthy index range back into weight gives the range:

Ideal weight range — in words: the healthy range is the body mass index range of 18.5 to 24.9, times the height in metres squared. lowest weight = 18.5 × height in metres²; highest weight = 24.9 × height in metres²; Devine ideal = 50 kg (men) or 45.5 kg (women) + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 feet

Formula source: the World Health Organization healthy body mass index range of 18.5–24.9, and the Devine formula for ideal body weight. Both are guides only — muscle, age and build change what a healthy weight means for one person.

4Worked example: 175 cm tall

  1. Start with the number: height 175 cm, which is 1.75 metres.
  2. Square the height: 1.75 × 1.75 = 3.0625.
  3. Lowest weight: 18.5 × 3.0625 = 56.7 kg. Highest weight: 24.9 × 3.0625 = 76.3 kg.
  4. Answer: the healthy range is 56.7–76.3 kg (124.9–168.1 lb). The Devine ideal for a man of 175 cm is 70.5 kg.

6Questions about ideal weight

What is a healthy body mass index?

The World Health Organization calls 18.5 to 24.9 healthy for most adults. Under 18.5 is underweight, 25 to 29.9 is overweight, and 30 or more is obese. These are ranges for groups, not a verdict on one person.

What is the Devine ideal weight?

It is an older formula from medicine: 50 kg for a man or 45.5 kg for a woman at 5 feet tall, plus 2.3 kg for each inch above 5 feet. It gives one number, while the index range gives a range — both are guides.

Why is it a range, not one number?

Because healthy weight depends on build, muscle and age. Two people of the same height can both be healthy at different weights. A range is more honest than one number.

Does muscle change the answer?

Yes. Muscle weighs more than fat, so a muscular person can sit above the range and still be healthy, while a person with little muscle can sit inside the range and still carry too much fat.

Can I type my height in inches?

This page takes centimetres. To change inches to centimetres, multiply the inches by 2.54 — for example, 69 inches is 175 cm.

Is this medical advice?

No. This is a general guide only, worked out from the number you type. It is not a diagnosis and never replaces advice from a doctor or another health professional.
