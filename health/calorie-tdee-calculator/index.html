2Quick answer

Your daily calorie need, or TDEE, is the calories you burn at rest plus the calories your activity adds. For example, a 30-year-old man of 70 kg and 175 cm who exercises 3 to 5 days a week needs about 2,556 calories a day. Type your own figures above and this calculator gives your daily need.

3The formula

The resting burn (BMR) comes from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, and the activity factor scales it up to a full day:

TDEE — in words: work out your resting burn from your weight, height, age and sex, then multiply it by your activity factor. TDEE = BMR × activity factor, where BMR = 10 × weight + 6.25 × height − 5 × age + 5 for men or − 161 for women

Formula source: the Mifflin-St Jeor equation (1990) for basal metabolic rate, with the standard activity factors 1.2, 1.375, 1.55, 1.725 and 1.9. This is a guide only, not medical advice and not a diet plan.

4Worked example: man, 30 years, 70 kg, 175 cm, moderately active

  1. Start with the figures: weight 70 kg, height 175 cm, age 30, male, activity factor 1.55.
  2. Work out the resting burn: 10 × 70 + 6.25 × 175 − 5 × 30 + 5 = 1,648.75, which rounds to 1,649 calories a day.
  3. Multiply by the activity factor: 1,648.75 × 1.55 = 2,555.56, which rounds to 2,556.
  4. Answer: the daily calorie need (TDEE) is about 2,556 calories a day.

Try it in the tool above: type 70, 175 and 30 with Male and “Moderately active” picked, and the result line shows 2,556.

6Questions about calorie tdee

What is TDEE?

Total Daily Energy Expenditure: all the calories you burn in a day, including your resting burn, moving around, exercise and digesting food. Eat about your TDEE to stay the same weight, less to lose weight and more to gain weight.

What is BMR?

Basal Metabolic Rate: the calories your body burns at complete rest, just to keep you alive — breathing, keeping warm and running your organs. It is the biggest part of your TDEE, and the BMR calculator on this site works it out on its own.

How do I pick my activity level?

Be honest about a normal week. Desk job with almost no exercise is sedentary (1.2); light exercise 1 to 3 days a week is lightly active (1.375); exercise 3 to 5 days a week is moderately active (1.55). Picking a higher level than you really do is the most common mistake.

How many fewer calories should I eat to lose weight?

About 500 fewer a day loses roughly half a kilogram a week for most people. The table under your result shows your TDEE less 500. Losing weight faster than that is hard to keep up and is best talked through with a doctor.

Is this a diet plan?

No. It is a guide to how many calories your body uses. What you eat, your health and any medical conditions all matter too. If you have a medical condition or take medicine, talk to a doctor before changing what you eat.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: whole numbers, no decimal places. The resting burn is rounded to a whole calorie first, and the daily need is the resting burn times the activity factor, rounded to a whole calorie, so the figures always agree.
