2Quick answer

Walking burns about 0.04 calories per step for each kilogram of body weight, scaled to a 70 kilogram person. At 10,000 steps and 70 kilograms, that is 400 calories. Type your steps and weight above and this calculator shows the calories burned instantly.

3The formula

A heavier body burns more calories for the same steps, because more weight is carried. Speed, hills and fitness also change the true figure, so this is an estimate.

Calories from steps — in words: multiply the steps by 0.04, then multiply by the body weight and divide by 70. calories = steps × 0.04 × (body weight in kg ÷ 70)

Formula source: the common walking estimate of 0.04 calories per step at 70 kg, scaled by weight, on the two numbers you type. This is a general guide only, not medical advice.

4Worked example: 10,000 steps at 70 kg

  1. Steps: 10,000.
  2. Calories per step at 70 kg: 0.04.
  3. Calories: 10,000 × 0.04 × (70 ÷ 70) = 400.
  4. Answer: 400 calories.

Try it in the tool above: type 10000 and 70, and the result line shows 400 calories.

6Questions about steps to calories

How many calories does 10,000 steps burn?

About 400 calories at 70 kilograms, 480 calories at 84 kilograms, and 340 calories at 60 kilograms. The figure grows with body weight, because a heavier body does more work with each step.

Does walking speed change the calories?

Yes. Faster walking burns more calories per step than a slow walk, and hills add more again. This page uses one steady walking pace, so treat the figure as an estimate.

Do the steps include all my walking for the day?

Yes, if the step count is for the whole day. Put the full day's steps in the box, and the answer is the calories for all of that walking.

What if I weigh less than 20 kg or more than 300 kg?

This page accepts weights from 20 to 300 kilograms and steps from 0 to 100,000 a day. Outside that range it shows a message instead of a result.

Is this medical advice?

No. This is a general guide only, worked out from the numbers you type. It never replaces advice from a doctor or another health professional.

Is this calculator free, and do I need to sign up?

Yes, it is free, and no sign-up is needed. Nothing is uploaded: the calculation runs in your browser on your own device, and the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
