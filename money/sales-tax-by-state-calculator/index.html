2Quick answer

Sales tax is charged on the price at the state or province base rate. A $100 purchase in California at 7.25% owes $7.25 in base sales tax, so the total is $107.25. Pick your state or province above and this calculator shows the base tax instantly. County and city tax may add more on top.

3The formula

Only the state or province base rate is used. In the USA, counties and cities add their own tax on top, so the tax at the till is often higher. In Canada the rate shown is the combined GST, PST or HST base rate for the province.

Base sales tax — in words: multiply the price before tax by the base rate for the state or province, then divide by 100. base sales tax = price × base rate ÷ 100

Formula source: USA state base rates, Tax Foundation January and July 2025 (California 7.25%, Texas 6.25%, New York 4%, Florida 6%, and the other states as listed in the picker); Canada combined GST/PST/HST rates (Ontario 13%, Quebec 14.975%, British Columbia 12%, Alberta 5%). Last checked 11 November 2026. County and city tax may add more.

4Worked example: $100 in California

  1. Base rate in California: 7.25%.
  2. Base sales tax: $100 × 7.25 ÷ 100 = $7.25.
  3. Total to pay: $100 + $7.25 = $107.25.
  4. Answer: $7.25 of base sales tax. County and city tax may add more on top.

6Questions about sales tax by state

Is this the full tax I pay at the till?

No. This is the state or province base rate only. In the USA, county and city tax is added on top, so the real rate is often 1 to 4 points higher. In Canada the rate shown is the combined rate, so it is the full rate for most goods.

Which states have no sales tax?

Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon have no state sales tax, so the base tax is $0.00. Alaska allows local sales tax, so some towns there still charge tax.

Why is California 7.25%?

California's rate is 6% state tax plus a 1.25% statewide county tax, which together make 7.25%. That is the lowest rate anywhere in California before city and district tax is added.

What is the rate in Quebec, 14.975%?

Quebec charges 5% GST plus 9.975% QST, which together make 14.975%. The QST is charged on the price plus GST, so the combined figure is shown here as one base rate.

Do some goods have a different rate?

Yes. Food, clothing, medicine and other goods can be taxed at a lower rate or not at all in some states. This page uses the standard base rate for general goods only.

Is this calculator free, and do I need to sign up?

Yes, it is free, and no sign-up is needed. Nothing is uploaded: the calculation runs in your browser on your own device, and the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
