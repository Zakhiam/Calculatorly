2Quick answer

A 20% deposit on a $300,000 home is $60,000, which leaves a loan of $240,000 to borrow. The deposit is the home price times the deposit percent divided by 100. A larger deposit means a smaller loan, a smaller monthly payment and less interest over the life of the loan.

3The formula

The deposit is a percent of the price. The rest is the loan you need to borrow:

Down payment — in words: multiply the home price by the deposit percent, then divide by 100. deposit = home price × deposit percent ÷ 100; loan = home price − deposit

Formula source: the standard arithmetic definition of percent. No rate is fetched from anywhere — you type the price and the percent, and the page uses only those two numbers.

4Worked example: 20% deposit on a $300,000 home

  1. Start with the numbers: home price $300,000, deposit percent 20%.
  2. Turn the percent into a share: 20 ÷ 100 = 0.20.
  3. Multiply the price by the share: $300,000 × 0.20 = $60,000.
  4. Answer: the deposit is $60,000.00, and the loan left to borrow is $300,000 − $60,000 = $240,000.

Try it in the tool above: type 300000 and 20, and the result line shows $60,000.00.

6Questions about down payment

How much deposit do I need?

It depends on the lender and the loan. 20% is the common target because it often avoids extra insurance, but many loans allow 10%, 5% or even less. A smaller deposit means a larger loan and a higher monthly payment.

What is the loan after the deposit?

The loan is the home price minus the deposit. On a $300,000 home with a $60,000 deposit, the loan is $240,000. This calculator shows that figure in the table under your result.

Does a bigger deposit save money?

Yes. A bigger deposit means a smaller loan, so the monthly payment is smaller and the total interest over the loan is smaller. It also leaves you owing less than the home is worth, which lenders like.

Can the deposit percent be over 100?

No sensible deposit is over 100% of the price — that would be more than the home costs. If you type a percent over 100, the calculator shows a clear message instead of a made-up number.

What if the deposit percent is zero?

A 0% deposit means a deposit of $0.00 and a loan for the full price. Some loans allow that, but the monthly payment is at its highest and the lender may add extra costs.

Is this financial advice?

No. This is an estimate for planning, from the numbers you type. Deposit rules change by lender and by country, so check with the lender before you act.
