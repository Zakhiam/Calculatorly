2Quick answer

Commission is the total sales multiplied by the commission rate, divided by 100. For example, sales of $5,000.00 at a rate of 10.00% earn $500.00 in commission. A higher rate, or higher sales, means more commission. Type your own sales and rate above to see what you would earn.

3The formula

Commission is a straight percent of the sales: the rate is the share of each sale that is paid as commission:

Commission — in words: multiply the total sales by the commission rate, then divide by 100. commission = sales × rate ÷ 100

Formula source: the standard arithmetic definition of percent (per hundred). No rate is taken from anywhere else — the commission rate is the one you type.

4Worked example: $5,000.00 of sales at 10.00%

  1. Start with the figures: total sales $5,000.00, commission rate 10.00%.
  2. Multiply the sales by the rate: $5,000.00 × 10.00 = $50,000.00.
  3. Divide by 100: $50,000.00 ÷ 100 = $500.00.
  4. Answer: the commission is $500.00.

Try it in the tool above: type 5000 and 10, and the result line shows $500.00.

6Questions about commission

How do I work out commission by hand?

Multiply the total sales by the commission rate, then divide by 100. For example, $5,000.00 of sales at 10.00% is $5,000.00 × 10 ÷ 100 = $500.00. This calculator does the same sum instantly as you type.

What is the difference between a fixed rate and a tiered rate?

A fixed rate pays the same percent on all sales. A tiered rate pays a different percent once sales pass a threshold — for example, 5.00% on the first $10,000.00 and 10.00% above it. This calculator uses one fixed rate, so for a tiered deal, work out each tier separately and add the results together.

Is commission paid on the sales before or after tax?

That depends on the agreement. This calculator uses the sales figure you type as it is, with no tax added or taken off. If your commission is paid on sales before tax, type the sales before tax; if it is paid after tax, type the sales after tax.

What if the sales are zero?

Then the commission is $0.00. That is a real answer, not an error: no sales means no commission. An error only appears if a box is left empty, the rate is outside 0% to 100%, or a number is above the limit.

Can the commission rate be more than 100%?

No, not in this calculator: the rate must be between 0% and 100%. A rate above 100% would pay more in commission than the sales themselves, which is not a normal commission agreement.

Is this calculator free, and do I need to sign up?

Yes, it is free, and no sign-up is needed. Nothing is uploaded: the calculation runs in your browser on your own device, and the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
