2Quick answer

A fixed mortgage and a variable mortgage with the same loan and term can cost very different amounts. On a $300,000 loan over 25 years, a 4.5% fixed rate costs $500,249.23 in total while a 4% variable rate costs $475,053.16, so the variable loan is cheaper by $25,196.07. Type your figures above to compare your two rates.

3The formula

The variable rate is held flat for the whole term in this comparison, so the two loans are compared on the same basis. In real life a variable rate moves, and that is the risk you take for a lower starting rate.

Total cost of a loan — in words: work out the monthly payment from the loan, the rate and the term, then multiply by the number of months. monthly payment = loan × monthly rate ÷ (1 − (1 + monthly rate)^−months); total = monthly payment × months

Formula source: the standard loan payment formula, on the four numbers you type. No live rate is used — you type both rates. The variable rate is assumed to stay at the rate you type for the whole term.

4Worked example: $300,000 over 25 years, fixed 4.5% against variable 4%

  3. Difference: $500,249.23 − $475,053.16 = $25,196.07.
  4. Answer: Variable is cheaper by $25,196.07, if the variable rate never moves.

6Questions about fixed vs variable mortgage

Why is the variable loan cheaper here?

Because its rate is lower: 4% against 4.5%. A lower rate means a lower monthly payment and less interest over the term. If the variable rate rose above 4.5%, the fixed loan would become the cheaper one.

What is the risk of a variable rate?

It can rise, and your monthly payment rises with it. A fixed rate cannot rise during the fixed term, so the payment is certain. This calculator holds the variable rate flat, so it shows the best case for the variable loan, not the worst case.

What if the variable rate rises later?

Then the real cost of the variable loan is higher than the figure shown here. Try the comparison again with a higher variable rate — for example 5% or 6% — to see how much room the fixed loan gives you.

Does this include fees?

No. Arrangement fees, valuation fees and early repayment charges are not part of this sum. Add any fee to the loan it belongs to when you compare the totals by hand.

What does the verdict sentence mean?

It names the cheaper loan and the difference in total cost over the full term. The cheaper loan is not always the right choice: a fixed rate also buys certainty, which this page cannot put a price on.

Is this calculator free, and do I need to sign up?

Yes, it is free, and no sign-up is needed. Nothing is uploaded: the calculation runs in your browser on your own device, and the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
