2Quick answer

To work out a sale price, find the amount saved and take it off the full price. For example, 25% off 80 saves 20, so the sale price is 60. Type any price and discount percent above and this calculator gives the sale price and the amount saved instantly, with the breakdown below.

3The formula

A discount is a share of the price taken off, so the sale price is what is left after that share is removed:

Sale price — in words: take the discount percent of the price, then subtract that saving from the price. sale price = price − (price × discount ÷ 100)

Formula source: the standard arithmetic definition of percent. No rate, price or outside data is used by this tool — only the two numbers you type, in your own currency.

4Worked example: 25% off 80

  1. Start with the price and the discount: price 80, discount 25%.
  2. Work out the saving: 80 × 25 ÷ 100 = 20.
  3. Take the saving off the price: 80 − 20 = 60.
  4. Answer: the sale price is 60.00, and you save 20.00.

Try it in the tool above: type 80 and 25, and the result line shows 60.00.

6Questions about discount

How do I work out a discount by hand?

Multiply the price by the discount percent and divide by 100 to get the saving, then subtract the saving from the price. For 25% off 80: 80 × 25 ÷ 100 = 20 saved, so 80 − 20 = 60. This calculator does the same sum instantly.

What is 20% off 50?

20% of 50 is 10, so the sale price is 50 − 10 = 40. A quick check: 10% of 50 is 5, so 20% is double that, 10.

Can the discount be more than 100%?

No. A discount over 100% would make the sale price negative, which has no meaning for a price, so this calculator does not accept a discount above 100%. A discount of 100% makes the item free: the sale price is 0.00.

What is the difference between a discount and a sale price?

The discount is the amount taken off, and the sale price is what you actually pay. On an 80 item with 25% off, the discount is 20 and the sale price is 60. This page shows both, so you can see each one.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 2 decimal places. For example, 50% off 49.99 is 24.995, which rounds to 24.99. The same rule is used in the answer, the bar and the table, so the figures always agree.

Does the discount include tax?

No. This tool works on the price you type, before or after tax, exactly as you type it. If the price on the shelf already includes tax, type that price; if you want the price before tax, work it out on the VAT calculator first.
