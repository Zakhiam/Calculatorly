2Quick answer

An amount of 100 at an exchange rate of 1.27 converts to 127.00. The rate is the number you type yourself — this site does not fetch live exchange rates, so check a current rate from your bank or a rate source and type it in. The converted amount is the amount times the rate.

3The formula

The exchange rate says how much one unit of the first currency is worth in the second currency. The sum is one multiplication:

Currency conversion — in words: multiply the amount by the exchange rate you enter. converted amount = amount × exchange rate

Formula source: the standard definition of an exchange rate. This site does not fetch live rates and holds no rate data — the rate is typed by you, and the page uses only the two numbers you type. A bank or card may charge a fee on top, so the amount you receive can be less.

4Worked example: 100 at a rate of 1.27

  1. Start with the numbers: amount 100, exchange rate 1.27 (typed by you — this site does not fetch live rates).
  2. The rate means 1 unit of the first currency is worth 1.27 units of the second currency.
  3. Multiply: 100 × 1.27 = 127.00.
  4. Answer: the converted amount is 127.00. The reverse rate is 1 ÷ 1.27 = 0.7874, so 127.00 converts back to 100.

Try it in the tool above: type 100 and 1.27, and the result line shows 127.00.

6Questions about currency converter

Does this site fetch live exchange rates?

No. This site never fetches a live rate. You type the rate yourself, and the page says so on the page. Check a current rate from your bank or a rate source, then type it in.

What rate should I type?

The rate for the two currencies and the day you mean, from your bank or a rate source. Rates move during the day, so a rate from last week can give a different answer from today's rate.

Why did my bank give me less than this answer?

Banks and cards often add a fee or use a worse rate than the headline rate. This calculator shows the plain sum with no fee, so the amount you receive can be less.

What is the reverse rate?

It is 1 divided by the rate you typed. At a rate of 1.27, the reverse rate is 0.7874 — one unit of the second currency is worth 0.7874 units of the first. The table under your result shows it.

Can the rate be less than 1?

Yes. A rate under 1 means one unit of the first currency is worth less than one unit of the second. For example, 250 at a rate of 0.85 converts to 212.50.

Is this financial advice?

No. This is a plain multiplication for planning, from the numbers you type. It is not a quote, and it is not a promise of what you will receive.
