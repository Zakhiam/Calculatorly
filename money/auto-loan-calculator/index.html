2Quick answer

A monthly car loan payment comes from the amount borrowed, which is the car price less your deposit, plus the yearly interest rate and the term. For example, a 25,000 car with a 5,000 deposit at 6% over 4 years costs 469.70 a month. Type your figures above and this calculator gives the monthly payment and the total interest.

3The formula

You only borrow the price less your deposit, so the payment is worked out on that smaller amount. The standard repayment formula is:

Monthly car loan payment — in words: subtract the deposit from the car price to get the amount borrowed, then apply the standard monthly loan payment formula to that amount. borrowed = price − deposit; M = borrowed × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n)

Formula source: the standard amortisation formula for a fixed-rate loan. The interest rate is typed by you — this site does not fetch live car loan rates. This is an estimate only, not professional advice.

4Worked example: 25,000 car, 5,000 deposit, 6% over 4 years

  1. Start with the price and deposit: car price 25,000, deposit 5,000, so the amount borrowed is 25,000 − 5,000 = 20,000.
  2. Work out the monthly rate: 6 ÷ 100 ÷ 12 = 0.005, over 4 years (48 months).
  3. Put the numbers in the formula: 20,000 × 0.005 ÷ (1 − (1.005)^−48).
  4. Answer: the monthly payment is 469.70. Over 48 months that is 22,545.63 in total, of which 2,545.63 is interest.

Try it in the tool above: type 25000, 5000, 6 and 4, and the result line shows 469.70.

6Questions about auto loan

Does a bigger deposit lower the monthly payment?

Yes, twice over: you borrow less, so the payment falls, and you also pay less interest in total. Try raising the deposit above and watch both the payment and the total interest fall.

What if the deposit is the whole car price?

Then you borrow nothing, so the monthly payment is 0.00 and there is no interest at all. The calculator shows that as a real answer, not an error.

Can the deposit be more than the car price?

No. A deposit bigger than the price has no meaning, so the calculator shows a clear message instead of a result. Lower the deposit or raise the price.

What does the payment not include?

It does not include insurance, road tax, servicing or any dealer fees, which are charged separately. It covers only the loan repayment and the interest on it.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 2 decimal places. Each line is rounded on its own for display, so multiplying the rounded monthly payment by the number of months can differ by a few cents from the total shown.

Is this the payment the dealer will charge?

Not exactly. Dealers round payments their own way and add fees, and may use a different day count. Use this figure to plan and compare; ask the lender for the exact payment before you sign anything.
