Questions about Money calculators

Are the money calculators free?

Yes. Every calculator on this site is free, with no sign-up and no payment at any step.

Do the money tools use live interest rates?

No. Where a rate is needed, you enter your own rate, and the page says so. This site does not fetch live rates.

Are the answers exact?

The answers follow the formula shown on the page, rounded as the page states. They are estimates for planning, not professional financial advice.

Is anything I type sent anywhere?

No. Every calculation runs in your browser on your own device. The numbers you type are not sent anywhere and are not stored.

Which money tool should I start with?

Start with the tool that matches your question: Mortgage for a monthly home loan payment, Discount for a sale price, or Budget 50/30/20 for a monthly plan.
