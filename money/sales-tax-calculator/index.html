2Quick answer

Sales tax is a percent added to the price before tax. For example, 8% sales tax on a price of 100 is 8, so the total to pay is 108. Type your own price and tax rate above and this calculator gives the sales tax and the total to pay instantly, in your own currency.

3The formula

Sales tax is a percent of the price before tax, added on top at the till:

Sales tax — in words: take the tax rate percent of the price, then add that tax to the price. tax = price × rate ÷ 100, and total = price + tax

Formula source: standard percent arithmetic. The tax rate is typed by you, because sales tax rates differ by state, province and city — this site does not guess your rate. Amounts are in your own currency.

4Worked example: 8% sales tax on 100

  1. Start with the figures: price 100, sales tax rate 8%.
  2. Work out the tax: 100 × 8 ÷ 100 = 8.
  3. Add the tax to the price: 100 + 8 = 108.
  4. Answer: the sales tax is 8.00 and the total to pay is 108.00.

Try it in the tool above: type 100 and 8, and the result line shows 108.00.

6Questions about sales tax

What is the difference between sales tax and VAT?

Sales tax is added at the till on top of the shelf price, so the price you see is before tax. VAT is usually already included in the shelf price in most countries. This page is for sales tax added on top; the VAT calculator covers tax taken out of a price that already includes it.

What sales tax rate should I type?

Type the combined rate that applies where you buy — state, county and city rates added together where they apply. Rates differ by place and by item, so check the seller or your state tax authority for the rate.

Is sales tax charged on everything?

No. Groceries, medicine and some other items are often exempt or taxed at a lower rate, depending on the state. This calculator does the arithmetic on the price and rate you type; whether the item is taxable is something to check separately.

How do I work out the price before tax from the total?

Divide the total by one plus the rate as a share. If the total is 108 with 8% tax, the price before tax is 108 ÷ 1.08 = 100. The VAT calculator on this site does that sum for you.

Can the tax rate be zero?

Yes. If the item is exempt or the place charges no sales tax, type 0 and the total to pay is exactly the price. The calculator shows that as a real answer.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 2 decimal places. For example, 8% tax on 49.99 is 4.00, so the total is 53.99. The same rule is used in the answer, the bar and the table, so the figures always agree.
