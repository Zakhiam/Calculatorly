Questions about Everyday calculators

Are the everyday calculators free?

Yes. Every calculator on this site is free, with no sign-up and no payment at any step.

Is anything I type sent anywhere?

No. Every calculation runs in your browser on your own device. The numbers you type are not sent anywhere and are not stored.

Does the password checker store my password?

No. The password is checked in your browser only. It is never sent, stored or logged anywhere.

Can I use these tools on my phone?

Yes. Every page is built mobile-first and works at 360 pixels wide, with no sideways scrolling.
