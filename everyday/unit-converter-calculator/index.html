2Quick answer

10 metres converts to 32.8084 feet, because 1 metre is 3.28084 feet. This page converts length, weight, volume, temperature, area and speed between metric and imperial units, using fixed conversion factors. No live data is used — the factors never change, so the answer is the same today as it was last year.

3The formula

Every unit in a kind is a fixed multiple of one base unit — metres for length, kilograms for weight, litres for volume. The sum goes through the base unit:

Unit conversion — in words: change the value into the base unit of its kind, then change it out of the base unit into the unit you want. Temperature uses its own formula instead. converted value = value × (from unit in base units) ÷ (to unit in base units); temperature: °F = °C × 9 ÷ 5 + 32

Formula source: the standard fixed conversion factors — 1 mile = 1.60934 km, 1 kg = 2.20462 lb, 1 US gallon = 3.78541 litres, and the same for the rest. Temperature uses the standard Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin formulas. No live data is used.

4Worked example: 10 metres into feet

  1. Start with the numbers: value 10, kind length, from metres, to feet.
  2. The factor is 1 metre = 3.28084 feet.
  3. Multiply: 10 × 3.28084 = 32.8084.
  4. Answer: 10 metres is 32.8084 feet. Back the other way, 32.8084 feet ÷ 3.28084 = 10 metres.

6Questions about unit converter

Why is the answer given to 4 decimal places?

Because conversions often need more precision than money does. 10 metres is 32.8084 feet, not 32.81 — the extra places keep the answer exact enough to work with, and the same 4-place rule is used everywhere on this page.

Which gallon does this use?

The US gallon of 3.78541 litres, which is the gallon used on most recipes and fuel figures in the USA. The UK imperial gallon is larger, at 4.54609 litres, and is not on this page.

Why is temperature different from the rest?

Because temperature scales do not start at the same zero. Length and weight are simple multiples, but Celsius and Fahrenheit are shifted as well as scaled, so the sum needs its own formula — and Kelvin starts at absolute zero.

Can I convert between kinds, like metres to kilograms?

No. A length cannot become a weight — the units measure different things. The calculator only converts inside one kind, and the from and to boxes always show units of the kind you chose.

What if the from and to units are the same?

Then the answer is the value itself — 10 metres is 10 metres. That is a real answer, not an error.

Is anything fetched from the internet for this?

No. The conversion factors are fixed numbers written into the page, and the whole sum runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
