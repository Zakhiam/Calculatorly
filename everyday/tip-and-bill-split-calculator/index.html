2Quick answer

To split a bill with a tip, work out the tip, add it to the bill, then divide the total by the number of people. For example, a bill of 60 with a 15% tip is 69 in total, so each of 3 people pays 23.00. Type your own figures above and this calculator gives each share instantly.

3The formula

The tip is a percent of the bill, and the total is shared out equally:

Tip and bill split — in words: take the tip percent of the bill, add it to the bill, then divide the total by the number of people. tip = bill × tip percent ÷ 100; each person pays = (bill + tip) ÷ people

Formula source: standard percent arithmetic. The tip percent is typed by you, because tipping customs differ by country and by service. Amounts are in your own currency.

4Worked example: bill of 60, 15% tip, 3 people

  1. Start with the figures: bill 60, tip 15%, 3 people.
  2. Work out the tip: 60 × 15 ÷ 100 = 9.
  3. Add the tip to the bill: 60 + 9 = 69 in total.
  4. Answer: each of the 3 people pays 69 ÷ 3 = 23.00, of which 3.00 is tip.

Try it in the tool above: type 60, 15 and 3, and the result line shows 23.00.

6Questions about tip and bill split

How much tip should I leave?

It depends on where you are: 15% to 20% is usual in the USA, 10% is common in the UK when service is not already included, and in some countries no tip is expected at all. Type the percent that fits your meal.

Is the tip shared equally too?

Yes, on this page. The tip is added to the bill first, then the total is divided equally, so each person pays the same share of the tip. The table shows the tip per person as well.

What if one person pays for everyone?

Type 1 for the number of people. The share is then the whole total: a bill of 45 with no tip is 45.00 for one person.

What if the bill already includes a service charge?

Then the service charge is already in the bill, so type 0 for the tip percent unless you want to add more. A 0% tip gives each person just their share of the bill.

What if the shares do not divide exactly?

Each share is rounded half-up to 2 decimal places, so the shares can add to a cent more or less than the total. For example, 100 with 20% tip split 3 ways is 40.00 each, which adds to 120.00 exactly; with other numbers a cent can be left over.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 2 decimal places. The tip, the total and each share are each rounded on their own, so the shares can differ by a cent from the total. The same rule is used in the answer, the bar and the table.
