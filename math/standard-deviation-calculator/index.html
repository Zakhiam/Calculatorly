2Quick answer

Standard deviation measures how spread out a set of numbers is around their mean. For example, the numbers 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9 have a mean of 5 and a standard deviation of 2.0000. Type your own numbers above, separated by commas, and this calculator gives the mean, the variance and the standard deviation.

3The formula

The standard deviation is the square root of the variance, so it is measured in the same units as the numbers themselves:

Standard deviation — in words: work out the mean, subtract it from each number, square each difference, average the squares to get the variance, then take the square root. standard deviation = √(average of (each number − mean)²)

Formula source: the population standard deviation, which divides by the count of numbers. It is the version used when your numbers are the whole set, not a sample of a bigger set. No outside data is used.

4Worked example: 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9

  1. Start with the numbers: 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9. There are 8 of them.
  2. Work out the mean: (2 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 5 + 5 + 7 + 9) ÷ 8 = 40 ÷ 8 = 5.
  3. Work out the variance: the squared differences from 5 are 9, 1, 1, 1, 0, 0, 4 and 16, which add to 32, and 32 ÷ 8 = 4. The variance is 4.0000.
  4. Answer: the standard deviation is the square root of 4, which is 2.0000.

Try it in the tool above: type 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9, and the result line shows 2.0000.

6Questions about standard deviation

What does standard deviation tell me?

How spread out the numbers are around their mean. A small standard deviation means the numbers are close together; a large one means they are spread out. For 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9 the mean is 5 and the spread is 2.0000.

What is the difference between population and sample standard deviation?

Population standard deviation divides by the count of numbers, and is used when your numbers are the whole set. Sample standard deviation divides by the count less one, and is used when your numbers are a sample of a bigger set. This page uses the population version, and says so on the page.

What if all the numbers are the same?

The standard deviation is 0.0000, because there is no spread at all. For example, 5, 5, 5, 5 has a mean of 5, a variance of 0 and a standard deviation of 0.

How many numbers do I need?

At least two. With one number there is no spread to measure, so the calculator shows a clear message instead of a result. There is no upper limit except that each number must be under one trillion.

What units is the answer in?

The same units as your numbers. If your numbers are test scores, the standard deviation is in test score points; if they are heights in centimetres, it is in centimetres.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 4 decimal places. For example, the standard deviation of 10, 20 and 30 is 8.1650. The same rule is used in the answer, the bar and the table, so the figures always agree.
