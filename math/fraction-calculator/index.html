2Quick answer

To add or subtract fractions, give them the same bottom number, then add or subtract the top numbers; to multiply, multiply straight across; to divide, flip the second fraction and multiply. For example, 1/2 + 1/3 is 5/6. Type both fractions above and this calculator gives the simplified answer and the decimal.

3The formula

Fractions are worked out with whole numbers on the top and bottom, then simplified by dividing both by their largest common factor:

Fraction sums — in words: to add or subtract, use a common bottom number; to multiply, multiply the tops and the bottoms; to divide, turn the second fraction upside down and multiply. a/b + c/d = (a×d + c×b) ÷ (b×d); a/b × c/d = (a×c) ÷ (b×d); a/b ÷ c/d = (a×d) ÷ (b×c)

Formula source: standard fraction arithmetic. Only whole numbers are accepted on the top and bottom, and the answer is always simplified. No outside data is used.

4Worked example: 1/2 + 1/3

  2. Give them the same bottom number: 1/2 is 3/6 and 1/3 is 2/6.
  3. Add the top numbers: 3 + 2 = 5, so the answer is 5/6.
  4. Answer: 1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6, which is about 0.83 as a decimal.

6Questions about fraction

How do I add fractions with different bottom numbers?

Give them the same bottom number first. For 1/2 + 1/3, use 6: 1/2 is 3/6 and 1/3 is 2/6, so the sum is 5/6. The calculator does this step for you.

How do I divide fractions?

Turn the second fraction upside down and multiply. For 1/2 ÷ 1/4, flip 1/4 to 4/1 and multiply: 1/2 × 4/1 = 4/2 = 2.

Why is the answer simplified?

Because 4/6 and 2/3 are the same amount, and the simplest form is easier to read. The calculator divides the top and bottom by their largest common factor: 4/6 becomes 2/3.

Can the top or bottom number be negative?

Yes. A negative top number makes the fraction negative, and a negative bottom number is moved to the top, so the answer is always shown with the sign on top. For example, 1/−2 is shown as −1/2.

Why can the bottom number not be zero?

Because dividing by zero has no answer in ordinary arithmetic. A fraction with zero on the bottom cannot be worked out, so the calculator shows a clear message instead of a result.

How is the decimal rounded?

The fraction itself is exact and is never rounded. Only the decimal shown under it is rounded, half-up to 2 decimal places: 5/6 is about 0.83. The fraction is the exact answer.
