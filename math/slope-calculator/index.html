2Quick answer

The slope of a line is the rise divided by the run: the change in y divided by the change in x between two points. For example, the line through (0, 0) and (2, 4) rises by 4 over a run of 2, so the slope is 4 ÷ 2 = 2.0000. Type your own two points above.

3The formula

Slope measures how steep a line is, and in which direction it runs. A positive slope rises to the right, a negative slope falls to the right, and a slope of zero is flat:

Slope — in words: subtract the first y from the second y to get the rise, subtract the first x from the second x to get the run, then divide the rise by the run. slope m = (y2 − y1) ÷ (x2 − x1); line equation y = mx + b

Formula source: the standard definition of the slope of a line between two points. No outside data is used — only the four numbers you type.

4Worked example: (0, 0) to (2, 4)

  1. Start with the two points: (0, 0) and (2, 4).
  2. Find the rise: 4 − 0 = 4.
  3. Find the run: 2 − 0 = 2.
  4. Divide the rise by the run: 4 ÷ 2 = 2.
  5. Answer: the slope is 2.0000, and the line equation is y = 2.0000x + 0.0000.

6Questions about slope

What does the slope tell me about a line?

It tells you how steep the line is and which way it leans. A slope of 2 means the line rises by 2 for every 1 it moves to the right. A slope of −1 means it falls by 1 for every 1 to the right. A slope of 0 means the line is flat.

What is the equation of the line in the table?

It is the line written as y = mx + b, where m is the slope and b is the y-intercept — the value of y where the line crosses the y-axis. For the points (0, 0) and (2, 4), the equation is y = 2.0000x + 0.0000.

What if the two points have the same x value?

Then the line is vertical, the run is zero, and the slope is not defined — dividing by zero has no answer. The calculator shows a clear message instead of a made-up number. A vertical line has no slope, but it does have an equation of the form x = a number.

What if the two points are the same point?

Then there is no line at all — one point cannot fix a direction. The run and the rise are both zero, so the calculator shows the same clear message as for a vertical line.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 4 decimal places. For example, a slope of 0.33333… rounds to 0.3333. The same rule is used in the answer, the bar and the table, so the figures always agree.
