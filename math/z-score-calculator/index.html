2Quick answer

A z-score is the value minus the mean, divided by the standard deviation. For example, a value of 85 with a mean of 75 and a standard deviation of 10 has a z-score of 1.0000, which means it sits exactly one standard deviation above the mean. Type your own value, mean and standard deviation above.

3The formula

A z-score turns a raw value into a distance measured in standard deviations, so values from different data sets can be compared on the same scale:

Z-score — in words: subtract the mean from the value, then divide the difference by the standard deviation. z = (value − mean) ÷ standard deviation

Formula source: the standard definition of the z-score in statistics. No outside data is used — only the three numbers you type.

4Worked example: value 85, mean 75, standard deviation 10

  1. Start with the figures: value 85, mean 75, standard deviation 10.
  2. Find the difference from the mean: 85 − 75 = 10.
  3. Divide the difference by the standard deviation: 10 ÷ 10 = 1.
  4. Answer: the z-score is 1.0000 — the value sits one standard deviation above the mean.

Try it in the tool above: type 85, 75 and 10, and the result line shows 1.0000.

6Questions about z-score

What does a z-score of 1 mean?

It means the value sits exactly one standard deviation above the mean. A z-score of 0 means the value equals the mean, a z-score of −1 means it sits one standard deviation below the mean, and a z-score of 2 means it sits two standard deviations above it.

What does a negative z-score mean?

It means the value is below the mean. For example, a value of 70 with a mean of 75 and a standard deviation of 10 has a z-score of −0.5000. A negative z-score is a real answer, not an error — it simply shows the value is on the low side.

Why is the standard deviation not allowed to be zero?

Because the formula divides by the standard deviation, and dividing by zero has no answer. A standard deviation of zero would mean every value in the data set is the same, so there is no spread to measure against. The calculator shows a clear message instead of a made-up number.

What is a z-score used for?

It is used to compare values from different data sets, and to find how unusual a value is. For example, a test score of 85 in a class with a mean of 75 and a standard deviation of 10 is a z-score of 1.0000, which can be compared with a score from a different test with a different mean and spread.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 4 decimal places. For example, a z-score of 0.33333… rounds to 0.3333. The same rule is used in the answer, the bar and the table, so the figures always agree.
