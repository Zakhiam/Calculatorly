2Quick answer

A dice roll picks a random number from 1 up to the number of sides, for each die, and adds the rolls together. For example, 2 six-sided dice can give any total from 2 to 12. This calculator rolls real random dice in your browser, and a fresh roll appears every time you change a number above.

3The formula

A fair die gives every side the same chance. This calculator uses the browser's secure random numbers, which are built for exactly this kind of job — not a fixed pattern:

Dice roll — in words: for each die, pick a whole number from 1 up to the number of sides, using secure random numbers, then add all the rolls together. total = roll 1 + roll 2 + … + roll n, where each roll is a whole number from 1 to the number of sides

Formula source: a uniform random whole number from 1 to the number of sides, drawn with the browser's built-in secure random numbers (crypto.getRandomValues). No result is stored or repeated.

6Questions about dice random

Are the rolls really random?

Yes. Each die is rolled with the browser's built-in secure random numbers (crypto.getRandomValues), which are designed so that no roll can be predicted from the ones before it. The rolls are not worked out from a fixed list, and nothing is stored.

Can I roll more than 2 dice, or dice with more sides?

Yes. You can roll 1 to 100 dice, each with 2 to 100 sides. For example, 1 die with 20 sides is a d20, the die used in many tabletop games, and 3 dice with 6 sides give a total from 3 to 18.

Why does the total change when I only click in a box?

Because every change rolls the dice again: the tool is live, and each new set of numbers means a fresh roll. If you want to keep a roll, write it down — the page does not store rolls, and going back will not bring the same one back.

Can this predict a roll, or help me win a game?

No. The rolls are random, and this page cannot predict them, influence them, or give an edge in any game. Any site that claims to predict dice rolls is not telling the truth.

What is the average total for 2 six-sided dice?

The average roll of one six-sided die is 3.5, so two dice average 7. Totals near the middle are more likely than totals at the ends: there is only one way to roll 2 (1 + 1), but six ways to roll 7.

Is this calculator free, and do I need to sign up?

Yes, it is free, and no sign-up is needed. Nothing is uploaded: the roll happens in your browser on your own device, and the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
