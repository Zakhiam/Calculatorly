6Questions about scientific

What order does the calculator work sums out in?

Brackets first, then powers and roots, then multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction. So 2+3*4 is 14, not 20, and (2+3)*4 is 20. Use brackets whenever the order is not clear.

Are angles in degrees or radians?

Degrees, as on most pocket calculators. sin(30) is 0.5, sin(90) is 1 and cos(60) is 0.5. If you need radians, the inverse functions (asin, acos, atan) give their answers in degrees too.

What does % do in a sum?

It means percent: 50% is 0.5, so 200*50% is 100. It is not the remainder operation that some programming languages use.

How do I type a power or a root?

Use ^ for a power: 2^3 is 8, and 2^-1 is 0.5. Use sqrt( for a square root: sqrt(16) is 4. Use log( for base 10 and ln( for the natural log: log(100) is 2 and ln(10) is about 2.302585093.

What if my sum cannot be worked out?

The calculator shows a clear message instead of a made-up number: a missing bracket, a word it does not know, dividing by zero, a log of zero or less, or a square root of a negative number all give a message that says what is wrong.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 10 decimal places, with trailing zeros cut off. For example, sin(30) is 0.5, not 0.5000000000. The same rule is used in the answer, the bar and the table, so the figures always agree.
