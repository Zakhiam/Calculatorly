2Quick answer

4Worked example: 20% of 100

  1. Start with the percent and the number: 20% of 100.
  2. Turn the percent into a share of 100: 20 ÷ 100 = 0.20.
  3. Multiply the share by the number: 0.20 × 100 = 20.
  4. Answer: 20% of 100 is 20.00 — and what is left of the number is 100 − 20 = 80.

6Questions about percentage of a number

How do I work out a percentage of a number by hand?

Divide the percent by 100, then multiply by the number. For example, 20% of 100 is 100 × 20 ÷ 100 = 20. This calculator does the same sum instantly and shows the working in the bar and table under your result.

What is the quick way to find 10% of a number?

10% is one tenth, so divide the number by 10 — or move the decimal point one place to the left. 10% of 250 is 25. From there, 5% is half of that (12.50) and 20% is double it (50).

Can the percent be more than 100?

Yes. A percent over 100 gives an answer bigger than the number itself, because you are taking more than the whole. For example, 150% of 40 is 60, and 200% of 40 is 80 — double the number.

What if the number is zero?

Any percent of 0 is 0.00, and 0% of any number is also 0.00. Those are real answers, not errors, so the calculator shows 0.00. An error only appears if a box is left empty or a number is above the limit.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 2 decimal places. For example, 10% of 33.33 is 3.333, which rounds to 3.33. The same rule is used in the answer, the bar and the breakdown table, so the figures always agree.

Is this calculator free, and do I need to sign up?

Yes, it is free, and no sign-up is needed. Nothing is uploaded: the calculation runs in your browser on your own device, and the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
