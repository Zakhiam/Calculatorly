2Quick answer

Concrete is ordered by volume, and the amount is the length times the width times the depth, plus waste. A slab 3 metres by 2 metres and 0.15 metres deep needs 0.99 m³ with 10% waste allowed. Type your measurements above and this calculator shows the volume, the bags and the weight.

3The formula

Bags are 25 kilogram bags, each filling about 0.0125 m³, and the number is rounded up. The weight uses 2,400 kilograms per cubic metre of concrete.

Concrete volume — in words: multiply the length by the width by the depth to get the volume, then add the waste allowance. volume = length × width × depth × (1 + waste ÷ 100)

Formula source: volume arithmetic on the four numbers you type, with 25 kg bags at 0.0125 m³ each and concrete at 2,400 kg per m³. Measure in metres. This is an estimate for planning a job.

4Worked example: 3 m by 2 m, 0.15 m deep, 10% waste

  1. Volume: 3 × 2 × 0.15 = 0.90 m³.
  2. With 10% waste: 0.90 × 1.10 = 0.99 m³.
  3. Bags: 0.99 ÷ 0.0125 = 79.2, rounded up to 80 bags of 25 kg.
  4. Answer: 0.99 m³. Weight: 0.99 × 2,400 = 2,376 kg.

Try it in the tool above: type 3, 2, 0.15 and 10, and the result line shows 0.99 m³.

6Questions about concrete

What is the waste allowance for?

Spillage, uneven ground and formwork that is not exact. A 10% allowance is normal for a home slab. Use 15% for rough ground or a shape with many corners.

How many bags do I need?

This page counts 25 kilogram bags, each filling about 0.0125 m³. For 0.99 m³ that is 80 bags. Check the bag size on the label — a 40 kilogram bag fills about 0.02 m³, so you would need half as many.

Can I use feet and inches instead of metres?

This page takes metres only. To change feet to metres, divide by 3.281. For example, 10 feet is 3.05 metres.

How heavy is the concrete?

About 2,400 kilograms per cubic metre. A 0.99 m³ slab weighs 2,376 kilograms, so plan the delivery and the barrow work for that weight.

What if my slab is not a rectangle?

Split it into rectangles, work each one out on its own, and add the volumes together. This page works out one rectangle at a time.

Is this calculator free, and do I need to sign up?

Yes, it is free, and no sign-up is needed. Nothing is uploaded: the calculation runs in your browser on your own device, and the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
