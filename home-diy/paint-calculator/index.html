2Quick answer

Paint is worked out from the area, the coats and the coverage on the tin. A 40 square metre wall with 2 coats at 12 square metres per litre needs 7.33 litres with 10% waste allowed. Type your figures above and this calculator shows the litres and the tins to buy.

3The formula

Tins are counted in 5-litre tins, rounded up. The coverage per litre is printed on the tin — check it, because it changes with the paint and the surface.

Paint needed — in words: multiply the wall area by the coats, divide by the coverage per litre, then add the waste allowance. litres = (wall area × coats ÷ coverage per litre) × (1 + waste ÷ 100)

Formula source: plain arithmetic on the four numbers you type, with 5-litre tins. No outside data is used. This is an estimate for planning a job.

4Worked example: 40 m², 2 coats, 12 m² per litre, 10% waste

  1. Paint without waste: 40 × 2 ÷ 12 = 6.67 litres.
  2. With 10% waste: 6.67 × 1.10 = 7.33 litres.
  3. Tins of 5 litres: 7.33 ÷ 5 = 1.47, rounded up to 2 tins.
  4. Answer: 7.33 litres.

Try it in the tool above: type 40, 2, 12 and 10, and the result line shows 7.33 litres.

6Questions about paint

How do I work out the wall area?

Measure each wall: width times height, in metres. Add the walls together, then take off the area of doors and windows. A door is about 2 square metres and a window about 1.5 square metres.

What is the coverage per litre?

It is printed on the paint tin, usually 10 to 14 square metres per litre for a wall. Rough or dark surfaces need more paint, so use the lower figure on the tin for those.

Why are there two coats?

Most walls need two coats for an even colour, and a colour change can need three. Put the coats you plan to paint in the coats box — the paint is counted for every coat.

What is the waste allowance for?

Paint left in the tray, on the roller and on the brush, plus spills. A 10% allowance is normal. Use 15% for a rough surface or a first coat on bare plaster.

How many tins do I need?

This page counts 5-litre tins, rounded up. For 7.33 litres that is 2 tins. If you buy a different tin size, divide the litres by that size and round up.
