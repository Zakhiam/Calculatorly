2Quick answer

Business days are Monday to Friday, and both end dates are counted. From Monday 2 November 2026 to Friday 6 November 2026 there are 5 business days. Type your two dates above and this calculator counts the business days instantly.

3The formula

Public holidays are not taken off here — use the Working Days Between Dates Calculator if you need holidays taken off too.

Business days — in words: count every day from the start date to the end date, counting both end dates, and take off Saturdays and Sundays. business days = days from start to end, both ends counted, minus Saturdays and Sundays

Formula source: the calendar, on the two dates you type. No outside data is used. Saturdays and Sundays are never business days on this page.

4Worked example: 2 November 2026 to 6 November 2026

  1. Total days, counting both end dates: 5.
  2. Weekend days in that range: 0.
  3. Business days: 5 − 0 = 5.
  4. Answer: 5 business days.

Try it in the tool above: pick 2 November 2026 and 6 November 2026, and the result line shows 5 business days.

6Questions about business days

Are both end dates counted?

Yes. The start date and the end date are both counted if they fall on a weekday. A range from Monday to Friday is 5 business days, not 4.

What about public holidays?

They are not taken off here. A public holiday on a weekday still counts as a business day on this page. Use the Working Days Between Dates Calculator, which has a box for holidays.

What if the range is only a weekend?

Then the answer is 0 business days. That is a real answer, not an error: a Saturday to Sunday range has no business days in it.

Can the start date be after the end date?

No. If the start date is after the end date, the page shows a message instead of a result. Swap the dates round and try again.

How far apart can the dates be?

From 1 January 1900 to 31 December 2100. Outside that range the page shows a message instead of a result.

Is this calculator free, and do I need to sign up?

Yes, it is free, and no sign-up is needed. Nothing is uploaded: the calculation runs in your browser on your own device, and the dates you type are not sent anywhere.
