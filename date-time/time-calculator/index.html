2Quick answer

This page does two time jobs at once. It counts the time between two times — for example, 8 hours 15 minutes lie between 09:30 and 17:45 — and it adds or subtracts hours and minutes from a time. Pick the times and type the hours and minutes above, and both answers appear with the working shown.

3The formula

Times are worked out in minutes after midnight, so the sums are plain subtraction and addition:

Time between two times — in words: turn both times into minutes after midnight, subtract, and turn the answer back into hours and minutes. To add or subtract, add or take away the minutes and wrap past midnight if needed. duration = end time − start time, in minutes; new time = start time ± hours and minutes

Formula source: time arithmetic on the times you type, on a 24-hour clock. Hours run 0 to 23 and minutes run 0 to 59. If the end time is earlier than the start time, it is counted as the next day.

4Worked example: from 09:30 to 17:45

  1. Start with the times: start 09:30, end 17:45.
  2. Turn both into minutes after midnight: 09:30 is 570 minutes, 17:45 is 1,065 minutes.
  3. Subtract: 1,065 − 570 = 495 minutes, which is 8 hours 15 minutes.
  4. Answer: the time between 09:30 and 17:45 is 8 hours 15 minutes. Adding 2 hours 30 minutes to 09:30 gives 12:00.

Try it in the tool above: pick 09:30 and 17:45 with 2 hours and 30 minutes to add, and the result line shows 8 hours 15 minutes.

6Questions about time

What if the end time is earlier than the start time?

It is counted as the next day. From 22:00 to 06:30 is 8 hours 30 minutes, because the end time falls on the next day. This is the usual way night shifts are counted.

How do I add hours to a time?

Type the hours and minutes and pick “Add to the start time”. The table under the result shows the start time plus the hours and minutes, wrapping past midnight: 23:30 plus 2 hours is 01:30.

Does this count seconds?

No. This page works in hours and minutes only, which is what work shifts, school days and travel times are usually counted in. Seconds are not accepted.

Can I work out a shift length that crosses midnight?

Yes. Pick the start time and the end time, and the page counts the end time as the next day when it is earlier. A shift from 22:00 to 06:30 is 8 hours 30 minutes.

Why is the answer in hours and minutes, not a decimal?

Because 8.25 hours is harder to read as a time than 8 hours 15 minutes. The table shows the answer the same way, so the figures always agree.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: whole numbers, no decimal places. Hours and minutes are whole counts, so there is nothing to round.
