2Quick answer

Working days are Monday to Friday, counting both end dates, with public holidays taken off. From Monday 2 November to Friday 6 November 2026 there are 5 business days, and with 1 public holiday taken off there are 4 working days. Type your dates and holidays above to count your range.

3The formula

Business days are Monday to Friday. The holidays box counts only holidays that fall on a weekday inside the range — a holiday on a Saturday or Sunday is already taken off as a weekend day.

Working days — in words: count the business days from the start date to the end date, counting both end dates, then take off the public holidays. working days = business days in the range − public holidays in the range

Formula source: the calendar and the holiday count you type. No holiday list is built in, because holidays differ by country and by year, so you type the number yourself.

4Worked example: 2 November to 6 November 2026, 1 holiday

  1. Total days, counting both end dates: 5.
  2. Weekend days in that range: 0, so business days are 5.
  3. Public holidays: 1.
  4. Answer: 5 − 1 = 4 working days.

Try it in the tool above: pick 2 November 2026 and 6 November 2026, type 1 holiday, and the result line shows 4 working days.

6Questions about working days between dates

How is this different from the Business Days Calculator?

This page has a box for public holidays, and takes them off the count. The Business Days Calculator counts Monday to Friday only, and leaves holidays in.

Should a holiday on a weekend be counted?

No. A holiday on a Saturday or Sunday is already taken off as a weekend day. Count only holidays that fall on a Monday to Friday inside the range.

What if there are more holidays than business days?

Then the page shows a message instead of a result, because the count cannot go below zero. Check the holiday number and try again.

Are both end dates counted?

Yes. The start date and the end date are both counted if they fall on a weekday and are not holidays.

How far apart can the dates be?

From 1 January 1900 to 31 December 2100. Outside that range the page shows a message instead of a result.

Is this calculator free, and do I need to sign up?

Yes, it is free, and no sign-up is needed. Nothing is uploaded: the calculation runs in your browser on your own device, and the dates you type are not sent anywhere.
