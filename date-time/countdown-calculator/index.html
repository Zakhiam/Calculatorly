2Quick answer

A countdown counts the time left until a date. From 11 November 2026 to 1 January 2027 there are 51 days, 0 hours, 0 minutes, 0 seconds left. Type your target date above and this calculator shows the time left, down to the second.

3The formula

The count is worked out when the page loads. Refresh the page to update the seconds. A target date in the past shows a message instead of a count.

Time left — in words: take the target date and time, take away the current date and time, and split what is left into days, hours, minutes and seconds. time left = target date and time − now, split into days, hours, minutes and seconds

Formula source: the clock on your own device and the date you type. No outside data is used, and nothing is sent anywhere.

4Worked example: 11 November 2026 to 1 January 2027

  1. Days left: 1 January 2027 minus 11 November 2026 = 51 days.
  2. Hours, minutes and seconds left on top of those days: 0, because both dates are counted from midnight.
  3. In weeks: 51 ÷ 7 = 7 weeks and 2 days.
  4. Answer: 51 days, 0 hours, 0 minutes, 0 seconds.

Try it in the tool above: pick 1 January 2027 and 11 November 2026, and the result line shows 51 days, 0 hours, 0 minutes, 0 seconds.

6Questions about countdown

Does the countdown tick every second?

No. It is worked out once, when the page loads. Refresh the page to see the seconds move on. The days, hours and minutes are right at the moment the page loads.

What if the target date is today?

Then the answer is 0 days, 0 hours, 0 minutes, 0 seconds, counted from midnight. A target date in the past shows a message instead of a count.

What is the second date box for?

It lets you count from a date other than today, for checking or planning. Leave it blank and the countdown starts from today.

How far ahead can I count?

To 31 December 2100. A target date after that shows a message instead of a result.

Does the countdown use my time zone?

Yes. It uses the clock on your own device, so the count is right for where you are.

Is this calculator free, and do I need to sign up?

Yes, it is free, and no sign-up is needed. Nothing is uploaded: the calculation runs in your browser on your own device, and the dates you type are not sent anywhere.
