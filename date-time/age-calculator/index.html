2Quick answer

Your exact age is the time from your date of birth to the date you measure to, counted in years, months and days. For example, someone born on 15 June 1990 is 36 years, 4 months and 27 days old on 11 November 2026. Pick both dates above and this calculator gives your exact age instantly.

3The formula

Age is counted in whole calendar years, months and days, not by dividing the total days by 365, because months and years are not all the same length:

Age — in words: count the full years from your birth date to the measuring date, then the full months left over, then the days left over. age = full years, then full months, then days, from the birth date to the measuring date

Formula source: calendar arithmetic on the dates you type, counting full years and months on the calendar, leap years included. Dates from 1900 onwards are accepted.

4Worked example: born 15 June 1990, measured on 11 November 2026

  1. Start with the two dates: birth 15 June 1990, measuring date 11 November 2026.
  2. Count the full years: from 15 June 1990 to 15 June 2026 is 36 years.
  3. Count the full months left: from 15 June 2026 to 15 October 2026 is 4 months, and from 15 October to 11 November is 27 days.
  4. Answer: the age is 36 years, 4 months, 27 days — 13,298 days in total.

Try it in the tool above: pick 15 June 1990 and 11 November 2026, and the result line shows 36 years, 4 months, 27 days.

6Questions about age

Why is my age in months and days, not just years?

Because two people born in the same year can be months apart in age. Years, months and days give the exact age on the measuring date, which is what forms, schools and official records ask for.

Does the calculator count leap years?

Yes. The count runs on the real calendar, so 29 February is counted in leap years. Someone born on 29 February 2000 is 26 years old on 28 February 2026 and turns 27 on 1 March 2026 in most legal counting, because there is no 29 February in 2026.

Can I work out my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Pick any measuring date on or after your birth date, in 1900 or later. For example, pick the date of a wedding or a deadline to see how old you will be then.

What if the birth date is after the measuring date?

That has no meaning as an age, so the calculator shows a clear message instead of a result. Pick a birth date on or before the measuring date.

How is the total in days worked out?

It is the plain count of days between the two dates, leap days included. From 15 June 1990 to 11 November 2026 that is 13,298 days.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: whole numbers, no decimal places. Years, months, days and totals are all whole counts, so there is nothing to round.
