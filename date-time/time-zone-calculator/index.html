2Quick answer

To find the time in another zone, take the time you have, work out the real offset of each zone on that date — including daylight saving — and shift the time by the difference. For example, 14:00 on 11 Nov 2026 in London is 09:00 the same day in New York, 5 hours behind. Pick your zones above.

3The formula

Time zones are not fixed numbers: the offset of a zone changes with daylight saving, so the conversion must use the offset that applies on the date you pick. This page uses the browser's built-in time zone data, which holds the real rules:

Time zone conversion — in words: turn the date and time in the first zone into one exact moment, then read that same moment in the second zone, using the real offset of each zone on that date. time in zone B = time in zone A + (offset of B − offset of A)

Formula source: the browser's built-in international time zone data (real IANA zones, with daylight saving). No offset is typed in or guessed — the offsets come from the browser itself.

4Worked example: 14:00 in London on 11 Nov 2026

  1. Start with the moment: 14:00 on 11 Nov 2026 in London (Europe/London).
  2. Find the offset of London on that date: Greenwich Mean Time, UTC+0, because daylight saving has ended.
  3. Find the offset of New York on that date: Eastern Standard Time, UTC−5, because daylight saving has ended there too.
  4. Shift the time by the difference: 14:00 minus 5 hours is 09:00.
  5. Answer: 14:00 in London is 09:00 the same day in New York.

Try it in the tool above: pick 11 Nov 2026, type 14:00, London to New York, and the result line shows 09:00.

6Questions about time zone

Why does the answer change with the date?

Because of daylight saving: zones move their clocks forward in spring and back in autumn, on different dates in different places. London and New York are 5 hours apart in both June and November, but other pairs change — Sydney and Los Angeles, for example, are 17 hours apart in January and 19 hours apart in July. Always pick the real date.

Where do the time zone offsets come from?

From the browser's built-in time zone data, which holds the real IANA time zone rules, including daylight saving. This page never guesses an offset and never stores a fixed list of offsets — it asks the browser for the offset that applies on the date you pick.

Can the converted time fall on a different day?

Yes, often. For example, 12:00 on 15 Jan 2026 in Sydney is 17:00 on 14 Jan 2026 in Los Angeles — the day before — because Los Angeles is 19 hours behind Sydney on that date. The sentence under the result always names both dates.

What if I type a time like 25:00?

The calculator shows a clear message instead of a result. Hours must be 00 to 23 and minutes 00 to 59, written as HH:MM — for example, 09:30 or 14:00.

Which time zones can I pick?

The list holds the zones most visitors need, from London, Paris and Berlin to New York, Toronto, Sydney, Tokyo and Auckland. If a zone you need is missing, pick the nearest city in the same zone — for example, Paris and Berlin share the same time.

Is this calculator free, and do I need to sign up?

Yes, it is free, and no sign-up is needed. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser on your own device, and the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
