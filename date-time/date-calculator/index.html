2Quick answer

This page does two date jobs at once. It counts the days between two dates — for example, 30 days lie between 11 November 2026 and 11 December 2026 — and it works out the date a number of days before or after a date. Pick the dates and type the days above, and both answers appear.

3The formula

Dates are counted on the real calendar, so month lengths and leap years are handled exactly:

Days between dates — in words: count the days from the first date to the second on the calendar, leap days included. To add days, move forward on the calendar; to subtract days, move back. days between = second date − first date, in days; new date = first date ± days

Formula source: calendar arithmetic on the dates you type, leap years included. Dates from 1900 to 2100 are accepted.

4Worked example: 30 days from 11 November 2026

  1. Start with the dates: first date 11 November 2026, second date 11 December 2026, and 30 days to add.
  2. Count the days between the two dates: from 11 November to 11 December is 30 days.
  3. Add 30 days to the first date: 11 November plus 30 days is 11 December 2026.
  4. Answer: there are 30 days between the dates, and 30 days after 11 November 2026 is 11 December 2026.

Try it in the tool above: pick 11 November 2026 and 11 December 2026 with 30 days, and the result line shows 30 days.

6Questions about date

Does the count include the start and end dates?

No. The count is the days from the first date to the second, so 11 November to 11 December is 30 days, not 31. If you need both end dates counted, add 1 to the answer.

Does the calculator handle leap years?

Yes. February has 29 days in leap years, so 28 February 2024 to 1 March 2024 is 2 days, while in a non-leap year it is 1 day.

Can I count days between past dates?

Yes. Pick any two dates between 1900 and 2100, in either order — the count is always the number of days between them, and it is never negative.

How do I work out a deadline 30 days from now?

Pick the start date, type 30, and pick “After the first date”. The table under the result shows the date 30 days after, and the date 30 days before, the first date.

What is the most days I can add?

36,500 days, which is about 100 years. Adding more than that is not accepted, because the result would fall outside the 1900 to 2100 range this page covers.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: whole numbers, no decimal places. Days and dates are whole counts, so there is nothing to round.
