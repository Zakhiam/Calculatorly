Questions about Date and Time calculators

Do date tools count weekends?

Tools that count working days skip Saturdays and Sundays. The page says which days it counts.

Can I count days between past dates?

Yes. Date tools work with past and future dates inside the range written on each page.

Are the date calculators free?

Yes. Every calculator on this site is free, with no sign-up.

Is anything I type sent anywhere?

No. Every calculation runs in your browser on your own device. The numbers you type are not sent anywhere and are not stored.
