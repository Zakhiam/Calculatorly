2Quick answer

Income tax in New Zealand is worked out on your salary in five bands. For example, on NZ$80,000 in 2026-27 the tax is NZ$16,277.50: 10.5% on the first $15,600, then 17.5%, 30% and 33% on the higher slices. The ACC levy is charged separately and is not part of this figure.

4Worked example: NZ$80,000 in 2026-27

  1. Start with the salary: 80,000.
  2. Split it into the bands: 10.5% of 15,600 = 1,638.00; 17.5% of (53,500 − 15,600) = 6,632.50; 30% of (78,100 − 53,500) = 7,380.00; 33% of (80,000 − 78,100) = 627.00.
  3. Answer: the income tax is 1,638.00 + 6,632.50 + 7,380.00 + 627.00 = NZ$16,277.50 a year, or NZ$1,356.46 a month.

6Questions about new zealand income tax

What are the five tax bands?

The 2026-27 bands are 10.5% to $15,600, 17.5% to $53,500, 30% to $78,100, 33% to $180,000 and 39% above $180,000. Each rate applies only to the slice of income inside that band, never to your whole salary.

Is the ACC levy part of my income tax?

No. The ACC earners' levy of 1.75% is charged on top of income tax, up to $156,641 of earnings. The take-home pay page shows both; this page shows the income tax only.

Does this include KiwiSaver?

No. KiwiSaver contributions are retirement savings, not tax, so they are not included here. The default employee rate is 3.5% from 1 April 2026.

What is the Independent Earner Tax Credit?

It is a tax credit of up to $520 a year for people earning between $24,000 and $48,000 who do not get other support. It is not included here, so if you qualify, your real tax is a little lower than the figure shown.

When does the New Zealand tax year start?

The tax year runs from 1 April to 31 March, not from 1 January. The figures on this page are for the 2026-27 tax year, which runs from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 2 decimal places. Each band of tax is rounded to cents first, and the total tax is the sum of those rounded bands, so the figures always agree.
