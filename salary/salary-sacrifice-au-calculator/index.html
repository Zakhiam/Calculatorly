2Quick answer

Salary sacrifice cuts your taxable income by the amount sacrificed, so you pay less income tax and Medicare levy. On a salary of A$100,000 with A$10,000 sacrificed, the tax saved is A$3,200.00. Type your salary and sacrifice above and this calculator shows the saving instantly, with the new take-home pay below.

3The formula

Tax on a salary uses the ATO resident rates for 2026–27 plus the 2% Medicare levy, with the levy shade-in for low incomes. The sacrificed amount goes into super, where it is taxed at 15% inside the fund.

Tax saved by salary sacrifice — in words: work out the tax on the full salary, work out the tax on the salary after the sacrifice, and take the second away from the first. tax saved = tax on full salary − tax on salary after sacrifice

Formula source: ATO resident tax rates 2026–27 (0% to A$18,200, 15% to A$45,000, 30% to A$135,000, 37% to A$190,000, 45% above) and the 2% Medicare levy. Last checked 11 November 2026. This is an estimate only, not professional advice.

4Worked example: A$100,000 salary, A$10,000 sacrificed

  1. Tax on A$100,000: income tax A$20,520.00 + Medicare levy A$2,000.00 = A$22,520.00.
  2. Tax on A$90,000 after the sacrifice: income tax A$17,520.00 + Medicare levy A$1,800.00 = A$19,320.00.
  3. Tax saved: A$22,520.00 − A$19,320.00 = A$3,200.00.
  4. Answer: tax saved A$3,200.00. Take-home pay falls from A$77,480.00 to A$70,680.00, and A$8,500.00 (the A$10,000 less 15% fund tax) goes into super.

6Questions about australia salary sacrifice

What is salary sacrifice in Australia?

It is an agreement with your employer to give up part of your salary in return for a benefit, most often extra super contributions. The sacrificed amount is not counted as taxable income, so you pay less income tax and Medicare levy on it.

How much of the sacrificed amount reaches my super?

This calculator takes 15% off inside the fund, so A$10,000 sacrificed puts A$8,500.00 into super. That is the standard contributions tax rate. A higher rate of 30% can apply to very high incomes under Division 293, which this page does not cover.

Do laptops, phones and tools still escape fringe benefits tax when salary sacrificed?

No. From 1 April 2027, work-related items provided through a salary sacrifice arrangement are no longer exempt from fringe benefits tax. That covers portable electronic devices such as laptops and phones, computer software, protective clothing, briefcases and tools of trade. The ATO published this change on 7 September 2026.

Does salary sacrifice change my super guarantee?

No. Your employer still pays the super guarantee of 12% on your ordinary earnings. The sacrificed amount is extra, on top of that. Check with your employer whether the guarantee is worked out on the salary before or after the sacrifice.

What is the Medicare levy shade-in?

Low incomes pay no levy, or a reduced levy. There is no levy on taxable income of A$28,011 or less, then the levy is 10% of the amount over that line until it reaches the full 2%. This calculator applies that shade-in.

Is this an exact figure for my tax return?

No. This is an estimate only, not professional advice. It uses the 2026–27 ATO rates, leaves out offsets, the Medicare levy surcharge, HELP repayments and Division 293, and it cannot know your full tax position. Check the ATO before acting on a figure.
