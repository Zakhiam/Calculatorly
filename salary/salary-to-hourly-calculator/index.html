2Quick answer

To turn a yearly salary into an hourly rate, divide the salary by the number of hours you work in a year. For example, 52,000 a year at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks is 2,080 hours, so the hourly rate is 25.00. Type your own salary, hours and weeks above and this calculator gives your hourly rate instantly.

3The formula

A yearly salary is pay for a whole year of hours, so the hourly rate is the salary shared out over those hours:

Hourly rate — in words: divide the yearly salary by the hours worked in a week, then divide again by the weeks worked in a year. hourly rate = yearly salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks a year)

Formula source: the standard salary-to-hourly conversion. No tax or other deduction is taken off here — this is the gross hourly rate, before tax. Amounts are in your own currency.

4Worked example: 52,000 a year at 40 hours a week

  1. Start with the figures: yearly salary 52,000, 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year.
  2. Work out the hours in a year: 40 × 52 = 2,080 hours.
  3. Divide the salary by the hours: 52,000 ÷ 2,080 = 25.
  4. Answer: the hourly rate is 25.00.

Try it in the tool above: type 52000, 40 and 52, and the result line shows 25.00.

6Questions about salary to hourly

How many weeks a year should I type?

Type 52 if you work all year round, or fewer if you take unpaid time off. A teacher on a 39-week contract types 39; someone with 4 weeks of unpaid leave types 48. Paid holiday still counts as weeks worked.

Is this my hourly rate before or after tax?

Before tax. This is the gross hourly rate from your gross yearly salary. To see what you keep after tax, use the take-home pay calculator for your country.

What if I work different hours each week?

Type your average hours per week. For example, if you work 30 hours one week and 50 the next, type 40. The answer is only as exact as the average you type.

How do I work out my hourly rate from a monthly salary?

Multiply the monthly salary by 12 to get the yearly salary, then use this calculator. For example, 3,000 a month is 36,000 a year, which at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks is 17.31 an hour.

Does overtime change my hourly rate?

No. Overtime pay is extra on top of your salary, so it does not change the hourly rate of the salary itself. Work out overtime separately from your hourly rate.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 2 decimal places. For example, 60,000 a year at 37.5 hours a week for 52 weeks is 30.77 an hour. The same rule is used in the answer, the bar and the table, so the figures always agree.
