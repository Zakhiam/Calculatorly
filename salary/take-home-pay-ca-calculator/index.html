2Quick answer

Take-home pay in Canada is your salary less federal income tax, Canada Pension Plan contributions and Employment Insurance premiums. For example, on 70,000 in 2026 the federal tax is 8,242.73, CPP is 3,956.75 and EI is 1,123.07, so the take-home pay is 56,677.45 a year. Provincial tax is not included, and Quebec is not covered.

3The formula

4Worked example: C$70,000 in 2026

  1. Start with the salary: 70,000.
  2. Work out the federal tax: 14% of 58,523 is 8,193.22, plus 20.5% of (70,000 − 58,523) = 2,352.79, less the basic personal amount credit of 16,452 × 14% = 2,303.28, so the federal tax is 8,242.73.
  3. Work out CPP and EI: CPP is 5.95% of (70,000 − 3,500) = 3,956.75, and EI is 1.63% of 68,900 = 1,123.07, because earnings above 68,900 are not insured.
  4. Answer: the take-home pay is 70,000 − 8,242.73 − 3,956.75 − 1,123.07 = C$56,677.45 a year, or C$4,723.12 a month.

6Questions about canada take-home pay

Is provincial tax included?

No. Each province and territory adds its own income tax on top of the federal tax, and the rates differ a lot, so this page covers federal tax, CPP and EI only. Check your province's rates separately and take them off the figure here.

What is CPP?

The Canada Pension Plan. Employees pay 5.95% of pensionable earnings from the $3,500 basic exemption up to the 2026 maximum of $74,600, and a second contribution, CPP2, of 4% on earnings from $74,600 to $85,000. Your employer pays the same amounts on top.

What is EI?

Employment Insurance. Employees pay 1.63% of insurable earnings up to the 2026 maximum of $68,900, which caps the yearly premium at $1,123.07. It funds benefits for job loss, parental leave and sickness.

Why is Quebec not covered?

Quebec runs its own pension plan (QPP) and parental insurance plan (QPIP), with different rates, and its EI rate is lower at 1.30%. The figures on this page are for the rest of Canada.

What is the basic personal amount?

It is a tax credit every filer gets: $16,452 in 2026, worth 14% of that, or $2,303.28, off your federal tax. For higher earners it falls gradually to $14,829 once net income reaches $258,482.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 2 decimal places. Each line — federal tax, CPP, CPP2 and EI — is rounded to cents first, and the take-home pay is the salary less those rounded lines, so the figures always agree.
