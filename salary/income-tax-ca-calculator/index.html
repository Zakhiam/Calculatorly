2Quick answer

Federal income tax in Canada is worked out at five marginal rates from 14% to 33%, less a credit for the basic personal amount. For example, on 70,000 in 2026 the tax on the brackets is 10,546.01, less a credit of 2,303.28, so the federal tax is 8,242.73. 3The formula

4Worked example: C$70,000 in 2026

  1. Start with the salary: 70,000.
  2. Work out the tax on the brackets: 14% of 58,523 = 8,193.22, plus 20.5% of (70,000 − 58,523) = 2,352.79, so the tax before credits is 10,546.01.
  3. Work out the credit: the basic personal amount is 16,452, and 14% of it is 2,303.28.
  4. Answer: the federal income tax is 10,546.01 − 2,303.28 = C$8,242.73 a year, or C$686.89 a month.

6Questions about canada income tax

What is the basic personal amount?

It is a tax credit every filer gets: $16,452 in 2026, worth 14% of that, or $2,303.28, off your federal tax. For higher earners it falls gradually to $14,829 once net income reaches $258,482.

What changed in 2026?

The lowest federal rate fell from 15% to 14% for the full year, and the brackets rose by 2% for inflation. The basic personal amount rose to $16,452. This page uses the 2026 figures.

What does marginal mean?

Each tax rate applies only to the slice of income inside its bracket. Moving into the 20.5% bracket does not mean you pay 20.5% on all your income — only the slice above $58,523 is taxed at 20.5%.

Is provincial tax included?

No. Each province and territory adds its own income tax on top of the federal tax, and the rates differ a lot, so this page covers federal tax only.

Are other credits claimed here?

No. Only the basic personal amount credit is claimed. Other credits, such as the Canada employment amount, are not included, so this page can overstate the tax a little.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 2 decimal places. Each line — the tax on the brackets and the credit — is rounded to cents first, and the federal tax is the rounded tax less the rounded credit, so the figures always agree.
