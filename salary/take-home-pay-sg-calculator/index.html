2Quick answer

Take-home pay in Singapore is your salary less your CPF contribution and your income tax. For example, on S$60,000 in 2026 the CPF contribution is S$12,000.00 and the income tax is S$1,110.00, so the cash take-home pay is S$46,890.00 a year. Your CPF money is savings in your own account, not lost pay.

3The formula

CPF is worked out on your salary, and income tax is worked out on your salary less CPF. The 2026 figures are:

Singapore take-home pay — in words: work out your CPF contribution, work out the income tax on what is left after CPF, and subtract both from your salary. take-home pay = salary − CPF contribution − income tax

4Worked example: S$60,000 in 2026

  1. Start with the salary: 60,000.
  2. Work out the CPF contribution: 20% of 60,000 = 12,000.00.
  3. Work out the chargeable income: 60,000 − 12,000.00 = 48,000. Work out the income tax on it: 2% of (30,000 − 20,000) = 200.00; 3.5% of (40,000 − 30,000) = 350.00; 7% of (48,000 − 40,000) = 560.00, so the tax is 1,110.00.
  4. Answer: the cash take-home pay is 60,000 − 12,000.00 − 1,110.00 = S$46,890.00 a year, or S$3,907.50 a month. The S$12,000.00 of CPF stays as savings in your own account.

6Questions about singapore take-home pay

What is CPF?

CPF (Central Provident Fund) is Singapore's mandatory savings scheme for retirement, housing and healthcare. Employees aged 55 or under put in 20% of their salary, and their employer adds 17% on top. The money stays in your own CPF account; it is not lost pay.

Is there a ceiling on CPF?

Yes. CPF is charged only on ordinary wages up to $8,000 a month ($96,000 a year) from 1 January 2026, and on total wages up to $102,000 a year. Above those ceilings, no more CPF is taken.

Do Employment Pass holders pay CPF?

No. CPF applies to Singapore citizens and permanent residents only. Foreign nationals on an Employment Pass, S Pass or Work Permit do not pay CPF, so their cash take-home pay is higher than the figure shown here.

When do I pay my income tax?

Singapore has no monthly PAYE withholding for most employees. You file once a year with IRAS (usually in March or April for the previous year) and pay the tax as a lump sum or in 12 monthly instalments.

Are tax reliefs included?

Only the CPF relief is included: your employee CPF contribution is taken off your salary before the tax is worked out. Other reliefs, such as the earned income relief of $1,000, are not included, so your real tax can be a little lower.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 2 decimal places. Each line is rounded to cents first, and the take-home pay is the salary less those rounded lines, so the figures always agree.
