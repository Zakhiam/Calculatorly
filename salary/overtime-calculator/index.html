2Quick answer

Overtime is paid on the hours over the standard week, at a higher rate. In the USA, 5 overtime hours at $25 an hour pay $187.50. In Australia the same week pays $325.00, because the first 2 overtime hours are time and a half and the rest are double time. Pick a country above to see your figure.

3The formula

In the USA the standard week is 40 hours and overtime is 1.5 times the hourly rate. In Australia the standard week is 38 hours: the first 2 overtime hours are 1.5 times the rate and later hours are double time.

Overtime pay — in words: count the hours over the standard week, then multiply them by the hourly rate and the overtime multiplier. overtime pay = overtime hours × hourly rate × multiplier

Formula source: USA — Fair Labor Standards Act, 1.5 times the rate after 40 hours a week. Australia — the common award pattern of 38 ordinary hours, time and a half for the first 2 overtime hours, then double time; your own award can differ, so check it. Last checked 11 November 2026.

4Worked example: $25 an hour, 45 hours, USA

  1. Standard week in the USA: 40 hours. Overtime hours: 45 − 40 = 5.
  2. Overtime rate: $25 × 1.5 = $37.50 an hour.
  3. Overtime pay: 5 × $37.50 = $187.50.
  4. Answer: $187.50. In Australia the same week would pay $325.00: 2 hours at $37.50 plus 5 hours at $50.00.

Try it in the tool above: pick United States, type 25 and 45, and the result line shows $187.50.

6Questions about overtime

When does overtime start in the USA?

After 40 hours in one working week, under the Fair Labor Standards Act. Those hours are paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate. Some states add daily overtime rules on top, which this page does not cover.

When does overtime start in Australia?

After 38 ordinary hours in a week under most awards. The first 2 overtime hours are usually time and a half, and later hours are double time. Your award can set different rates, so check it before relying on the figure.

Does overtime count for salary workers?

Usually not. Salaried workers are often paid a fixed amount whatever hours they work, unless their contract or award gives overtime. This calculator is for hourly-paid work.

What if I worked less than the standard week?

Then there is no overtime, and the answer is $0.00. That is a real answer, not an error: you were paid for your ordinary hours only.

Is overtime pay taxed at a higher rate?

No. Overtime is taxed at the same rates as ordinary pay. A bigger pay packet can push some income into a higher tax band, but the overtime itself is not taxed at a special rate.
