2Quick answer

Take-home pay in New Zealand is your salary less income tax and the ACC earners' levy. For example, on NZ$80,000 in 2026-27 the income tax is NZ$16,277.50 and the ACC levy is NZ$1,400.00, so the take-home pay is NZ$62,322.50 a year. KiwiSaver is not included here.

3The formula

Income tax and the ACC levy are both worked out on your salary. The 2026-27 figures are:

New Zealand take-home pay — in words: work out the income tax on your salary, work out the ACC earners' levy on your salary, and subtract both from your salary. take-home pay = salary − income tax − ACC earners' levy

Country: New Zealand. Tax year 2026-27 (1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027). Source: Inland Revenue (IRD). Last checked 11 November 2026. This is an estimate only, not professional advice. Income tax: 10.5% to $15,600; 17.5% to $53,500; 30% to $78,100; 33% to $180,000; 39% above. ACC earners' levy: 1.75% of earnings up to $156,641 a year. KiwiSaver, the Independent Earner Tax Credit and student loan repayments are not included.

4Worked example: NZ$80,000 in 2026-27

  1. Start with the salary: 80,000.
  2. Work out the income tax: 10.5% of 15,600 = 1,638.00; 17.5% of (53,500 − 15,600) = 6,632.50; 30% of (78,100 − 53,500) = 7,380.00; 33% of (80,000 − 78,100) = 627.00, so the tax is 16,277.50.
  3. Work out the ACC levy: 1.75% of 80,000 = 1,400.00.
  4. Answer: the take-home pay is 80,000 − 16,277.50 − 1,400.00 = NZ$62,322.50 a year, or NZ$5,193.54 a month.

Try it in the tool above: type 80000, and the result line shows NZ$62,322.50.

6Questions about new zealand take-home pay

What is the ACC earners' levy?

It is a levy that funds New Zealand's accident compensation scheme. Employees pay 1.75% of their earnings in 2026-27, up to $156,641 of earnings, so the most you pay is $2,741.22 a year. It is taken from your pay with your income tax.

Is KiwiSaver taken out of my take-home pay?

Not here. KiwiSaver is a retirement savings scheme, and the default employee rate rose to 3.5% from 1 April 2026. If you are enrolled, that amount comes off your pay on top of the tax and ACC levy shown here.

What is the Independent Earner Tax Credit?

It is a tax credit of up to $520 a year for people earning between $24,000 and $48,000 who do not get other support. It is not included here, so if you qualify, your real take-home pay is a little higher than the figure shown.

Does this include student loan repayments?

No. Student loan repayments of 12% on income over $24,128 a year are taken from your pay separately, so they are not included here. Take them off the take-home pay shown here yourself if they apply to you.

When does the New Zealand tax year start?

The tax year runs from 1 April to 31 March, not from 1 January. The figures on this page are for the 2026-27 tax year, which runs from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 2 decimal places. Each line is rounded to cents first, and the take-home pay is the salary less those rounded lines, so the figures always agree.
