2Quick answer

Federal income tax in the USA is worked out on your salary less the standard deduction, at seven marginal rates from 10% to 37%. For example, a single filer on 60,000 in 2026 has 43,900 of taxable income after the 16,100 standard deduction, and owes 5,020 of federal tax. Type your salary and filing status above for your own figure.

3The formula

4Worked example: single filer on $60,000 in 2026

  1. Start with the salary and status: 60,000, single. The standard deduction is 16,100, so the taxable income is 60,000 − 16,100 = 43,900.
  2. Work out the tax on the first bracket: 10% of 12,400 = 1,240.
  3. Work out the tax on the next bracket: 12% of (43,900 − 12,400) = 12% of 31,500 = 3,780.
  4. Answer: the federal income tax is 1,240 + 3,780 = $5,020.00 a year, or $418.33 a month.

Try it in the tool above: type 60000 with Single picked, and the result line shows $5,020.00.

6Questions about us income tax

What is taxable income?

It is your salary less the standard deduction (or your itemised deductions, if you itemise). On a 60,000 salary as a single filer, the 2026 standard deduction of 16,100 leaves 43,900 of taxable income, and the tax is worked out on that 43,900 only.

What does marginal mean?

Each tax rate applies only to the income inside its bracket. Moving into the 22% bracket does not mean you pay 22% on all your income — only the slice above 50,400 is taxed at 22%. Your effective rate is always lower than your top bracket.

Is state tax included?

No. State tax rates differ by state, and some states charge no income tax at all, so this page covers federal income tax only.

What is the standard deduction?

It is the amount taken off your salary before any tax is worked out: $16,100 for a single filer, $32,200 for a married couple filing jointly and $24,150 for a head of household in 2026. If you itemise deductions instead, this page does not cover that.

Does this include FICA?

No. FICA — Social Security and Medicare — is a separate payroll tax. To see your take-home pay after both federal tax and FICA, use the US take-home pay calculator.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 2 decimal places. The tax is rounded to cents, and the monthly figure is the yearly tax divided by 12, rounded the same way, so the figures always agree.
