2Quick answer

Take-home pay in Ireland is your salary less income tax, the Universal Social Charge and PRSI. For example, on €50,000 in 2026 the income tax is €7,200.00, the USC is €1,032.82 and PRSI is €2,100.00, so the take-home pay is €39,667.18 a year. The figures are for a single employee.

3The formula

Income tax, USC and PRSI are all worked out on your salary. The 2026 figures are:

Ireland take-home pay — in words: work out the income tax, work out the Universal Social Charge, work out PRSI, and subtract all three from your salary. take-home pay = salary − income tax − USC − PRSI

Country: Ireland. Tax year 2026 (calendar year). Source: Revenue (revenue.ie). Last checked 11 November 2026. This is an estimate only, not professional advice. Income tax for a single person: 20% to €44,000; 40% above; less tax credits of €4,000 (personal €2,000 plus employee €2,000). Universal Social Charge: no USC on income of €13,000 or less; then 0.5% to €12,012, 2% to €28,700, 3% to €70,044, 8% above. PRSI Class A: 4.2% of salary; none if you earn €352 a week or less (€18,304 a year). PRSI rises to 4.35% from 1 October 2026; this page uses 4.2% for the full year.

4Worked example: €50,000 in 2026

  1. Start with the salary: 50,000.
  2. Work out the income tax: 20% of 44,000 = 8,800.00; 40% of (50,000 − 44,000) = 2,400.00; total 11,200.00 less the €4,000.00 of tax credits, so the tax is 7,200.00.
  3. Work out the USC: 0.5% of 12,012 = 60.06; 2% of (28,700 − 12,012) = 333.76; 3% of (50,000 − 28,700) = 639.00, so the USC is 1,032.82.
  4. Work out PRSI: 4.2% of 50,000 = 2,100.00.
  5. Answer: the take-home pay is 50,000 − 7,200.00 − 1,032.82 − 2,100.00 = €39,667.18 a year, or €3,305.60 a month.

6Questions about ireland take-home pay

What are the tax credits?

Every employee gets a personal tax credit of €2,000 and an employee tax credit of €2,000 in 2026, €4,000 in total. They are taken off your tax bill, not off your salary, so a single person on €50,000 pays €7,200 of tax instead of €11,200.

What is the Universal Social Charge?

USC is a separate charge on your gross income. You pay nothing if you earn €13,000 or less in 2026, then 0.5% on the first €12,012, 2% to €28,700, 3% to €70,044 and 8% above that. No credits apply to it.

What is PRSI?

PRSI (Pay Related Social Insurance) builds your entitlement to social welfare payments and the State Pension. Most employees pay Class A PRSI of 4.2% of their salary in 2026, rising to 4.35% from 1 October 2026. This page uses 4.2% for the full year.

What if I am married or a one-parent family?

The cut-off point is higher: €53,000 for a married couple with one income, and €48,000 for a one-parent family. This page is for a single person, so if that is not you, your real tax is lower than the figure shown.

Do I pay PRSI if I earn very little?

No. If you earn €352 a week or less (€18,304 a year), you pay no employee PRSI at all. Above that, PRSI is charged on all of your earnings.

How are the answers rounded?

Every result on this page uses one rounding rule: half-up to 2 decimal places. Each line is rounded to cents first, and the take-home pay is the salary less those rounded lines, so the figures always agree.
