Questions about Salary and Tax calculators

Why is there a separate page for each country?

Tax rules, tax years, currencies and official sources are different in the UK, USA, Australia and Canada, so each country gets its own page with its own figures.

Which tax year do the salary pages use?

Each country page states its tax year, its official source and the date it was last checked, on the page itself.

Are the salary answers exact?

No. They are estimates from the rates shown on the page, for planning only. They are not professional tax advice. Check the official source before acting on a figure.

What currency does each page use?

UK pages use pounds (£), USA pages use dollars ($), Australia pages use Australian dollars (A$) and Canada pages use Canadian dollars (C$).

Do I need to sign up to use a salary calculator?

No. There is no sign-up anywhere on this site, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.
