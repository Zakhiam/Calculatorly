2Quick answer

Parental leave pay is your weekly salary, times the weeks of leave, times the pay rate. A $60,000 salary for 12 weeks at full pay gives $13,846.15. Type your salary, weeks and rate above and this calculator shows the total pay for the leave instantly.

3The formula

The pay rate is a percent of your normal salary: 100% is full pay, 50% is half pay. Unpaid weeks are not counted here — put only the paid weeks in the weeks box.

Parental leave pay — in words: divide the yearly salary by 52 to get the weekly pay, multiply by the weeks of leave, then multiply by the pay rate. leave pay = (yearly salary ÷ 52) × weeks of leave × (pay rate ÷ 100)

Formula source: plain arithmetic on the three numbers you type, with 52 weeks in a year. No government scheme, rate table or outside data is used. Government parental leave schemes pay their own fixed amounts, which are not part of this sum.

4Worked example: $60,000 salary, 12 weeks at full pay

  1. Weekly pay: $60,000 ÷ 52 = $1,153.85.
  2. Pay for 12 weeks at full pay: $1,153.85 × 12 = $13,846.15.
  3. Pay rate: 100%, so nothing is taken off.
  4. Answer: $13,846.15 for the 12 weeks.

Try it in the tool above: type 60000, 12 and 100, and the result line shows $13,846.15.

6Questions about parental leave pay

Is this the government parental leave payment?

No. This works out pay from your employer at a percent of your salary. Government schemes — such as Parental Leave Pay in Australia, Statutory Maternity Pay in the UK, and state schemes in the USA — pay their own fixed amounts on top of, or instead of, employer pay.

What if part of my leave is at half pay?

Work each part out on its own and add them together. For example, 6 weeks at 100% plus 6 weeks at 50% is two sums with the same salary and different weeks and rates.

What if my salary changes during the leave?

Use the salary that applies to the weeks you are counting. If a pay rise falls inside the leave, split the leave at the rise date and work each part out with its own salary.

Does this include tax?

No. It works out the gross pay during the leave, before tax. Tax is taken off by your employer in the normal way.

What is the most weeks I can count?

52 weeks, or one full year. A longer break has unpaid weeks, which this page does not count — put only the paid weeks in the weeks box.

Is this calculator free, and do I need to sign up?

Yes, it is free, and no sign-up is needed. Nothing is uploaded: the calculation runs in your browser on your own device, and the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
