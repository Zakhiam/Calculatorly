2Quick answer

Shift pay adds each part of the shift at its own rate. A shift of 8 day hours at $20 and 4 night hours at $30 pays $280.00 in total. Type your hours and rates above and this calculator shows the total for the shift instantly.

3The formula

Each part of the shift is paid at its own rate, so night and weekend hours can pay more than day hours. Put 0 in a part you did not work.

Shift pay — in words: multiply the hours by the rate for day, night and weekend work, then add the three parts together. total pay = (day hours × day rate) + (night hours × night rate) + (weekend hours × weekend rate)

Formula source: plain arithmetic on the six numbers you type. No rate, tax table or outside data is used. Tax is not taken off.

4Worked example: 8 day hours at $20, 4 night hours at $30

  1. Day pay: 8 × $20 = $160.00.
  2. Night pay: 4 × $30 = $120.00.
  3. Weekend pay: 0 × $40 = $0.00.
  4. Answer: $160.00 + $120.00 + $0.00 = $280.00.

Try it in the tool above: type 8, 20, 4, 30, 0 and 40, and the result line shows $280.00.

6Questions about shift pay

What counts as night work?

That depends on your contract or award. A common night window is 10 pm to 6 am, but your own agreement can set other hours. Put the hours that fall inside your night window in the night boxes.

What if my shift crosses midnight?

Split it by hand first: count the hours before and after midnight, then put the day and night hours in the right boxes. This page does not split a shift across midnight for you.

What if I worked a public holiday?

Use the weekend rate box for the holiday rate if your agreement pays a higher rate for it, or add the holiday hours at the holiday rate in the weekend boxes. Say which rate you used, so the total is clear.

Does this take tax off?

No. It works out the gross pay for the shift, before tax. Tax depends on your full income and your country, so it is not taken off here.

Can I use this for a whole week?

Yes. Put the whole week's day, night and weekend hours in the boxes and the total is the week's gross pay. Overtime is not added here — use the Overtime Calculator for that.

Is this calculator free, and do I need to sign up?

Yes, it is free, and no sign-up is needed. Nothing is uploaded: the calculation runs in your browser on your own device, and the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
