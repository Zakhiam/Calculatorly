2Quick answer

Statutory redundancy pay in the UK is worked out year by year: half a week for each year under 22, one week for each year from 22 to 40, and one and a half weeks for each year at 41 or over. A 45-year-old with 10 years of service and £600 a week is owed £7,200.00.

3The formula

Only the last 20 years of service count, and you need at least 2 full years to qualify. The weekly pay cap is £751 for redundancies on or after 6 April 2026 in England, Scotland and Wales.

Statutory redundancy pay — in words: count back from your age, give each complete year of service the weeks for the age you were in that year, add the weeks, and multiply by your weekly pay, capped at £751. redundancy pay = weeks of pay × weekly pay (capped at £751)

Formula source: GOV.UK statutory redundancy pay — 0.5 week per year under 22, 1 week per year aged 22 to 40, 1.5 weeks per year aged 41 or over, 20-year cap, weekly pay cap £751 from 6 April 2026. Last checked 11 November 2026. This is an estimate only, not professional advice.

4Worked example: age 45, 10 years, £600 a week

  1. Count back 10 years from age 45: ages 45, 44, 43 and 42 give 4 years at 1.5 weeks = 6 weeks.
  2. Ages 41, 40, 39, 38, 37 and 36 give 6 years at 1 week = 6 weeks.
  3. Total weeks: 6 + 6 = 12 weeks. Weekly pay £600 is under the £751 cap, so it is used in full.
  4. Answer: 12 × £600 = £7,200.00.

Try it in the tool above: type 45, 10 and 600, and the result line shows £7,200.00.

6Questions about redundancy pay

Do I qualify for statutory redundancy pay?

You need at least 2 full years of continuous service with the same employer. With less than 2 years there is no statutory payment, though your contract can still give you something.

Why is the answer worked out year by year?

Because the weeks depend on the age you were during each year of service, not the age you are now. A year worked at 44 counts 1.5 weeks, while a year worked at 30 counts 1 week, even for the same person.

What is the weekly pay cap?

For redundancies on or after 6 April 2026 the weekly pay used is capped at £751 in England, Scotland and Wales. If you earn more than that a week, the cap is used instead of your real pay. The most anyone can get is £22,530.

Is redundancy pay taxed?

The first £30,000 of a genuine redundancy payment is free of income tax. Any amount over £30,000 is taxed in the normal way. This calculator shows the gross amount before tax.

Does this cover Northern Ireland?

No. Northern Ireland has its own cap of £783 a week and a maximum of £23,490. This page uses the Great Britain figures of £751 and £22,530.

My employer offered more than this. Is that normal?

Yes. This is the legal minimum only. Many employers pay an enhanced package under the contract or a settlement agreement, and that can be much more than the statutory amount.
